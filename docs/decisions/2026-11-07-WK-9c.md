# WK-9c decisions (opening sealed worker records at the coordinator)

Runner's slice, 2026-11-07: the sealed-record open that WK-9b's brief (item 5) left until WK-8 (#572) merged, plus the follow-ups from #571's delta review. Builds on WK-8 (`2026-11-07-WK-8.md`: `WireKeys`, `SealedRecord`, the `SEALED` flag) and WK-9b (`2026-11-07-WK-9b.md`).

## Sealed records (`crates/pipeline/src/remote.rs` `RemoteIntake::take`)
- **Every record is opened before anything else is done with it.** `take` decodes with `ResultsBatch::decode_with`. Each sealed record is opened with `WireKeys::open` under the authenticated session's worker id, never one the payload names. What opens goes through the same fencing, purge check, rebuild and re-judge as before, and the commit's own `rejudge` decides. The worker's verdict, sealed inside, plays no part, as WK-8 says.
- **What doesn't open is refused alone and counted** as `remote_records_total{outcome="unopened"}`:
  - an unknown or expired key id;
  - a seal that fails under this session's worker id, whether tampered or sealed for another worker;
  - the `SEALED` flag over bytes that aren't a seal;
  - an inside that isn't exactly the clear header's one record.

  Its lease ends once as `Invalid`, holder-checked like any refused record, so the key isn't stranded. Nothing of it is logged.
- **A record in clear from a remote worker is refused and counted** as `remote_records_total{outcome="clear"}`. Every remote record is sealed (WK-8), and only the built-in worker, which never comes through the intake, sends records in clear. So this is a worker that misbehaves: a signal for WK-13. Its lease ends as `Invalid` the same way. This is fail closed.
- **A sealed record without its flag doesn't parse,** as WK-8's codec reads it: the flags then say "in clear", and what follows isn't a record. So the whole batch is undecodable, as any batch that doesn't decode: it's answered and dropped, and its leases last while the worker heartbeats (WK-9).
- **The flag must agree with what opens.** The codec takes a record as sealed only with flags exactly `SEALED` (WK-8). `open` takes only a seal that authenticates under the header and the worker, whose inside is one record in clear matching the header. So neither side's claim is trusted alone.
- **The keys:** `SinkParts::keys`, an `Arc<WireKeys>` that `cluster::intake::attach` creates and keeps on `Attached` (`Attached::keys`). **For WK-10:** share this `Arc` with `WorkerSnapshots::keys()` and set the wire keys at coordinator start (WK-8's note). Until then the keys are empty, nothing opens, and every remote record is refused (fail closed). WK-10 also builds the worker runtime that sends sealed records, so before it nothing is lost.
- **Tests (each fails with its check removed):**
  - `remote_intake.rs` `only_records_that_open_are_taken` sends, in one rig:
    - a valid sealed record (stored);
    - a wrong key, and a key id the coordinator lacks;
    - a tampered seal;
    - the flag over a plain record's bytes;
    - a record in clear;
    - one sealed for another worker;
    - one with its flag cleared (undecodable).

    Each refusal is counted, and its lease ends, except the undecodable batch's.
  - `a_sealed_quarantine_reaches_the_quarantine_sealed`, end to end (WK-8's last hop): a worker's sealed record the commit's re-judge quarantines (CS-2a) lands in `Store::quarantined` with its name sealed under the coordinator's own key, and the name is in no file of the data folder in clear.
  - Every existing intake test now sends sealed records, as a worker does. In `stale_and_guessed_tokens_commit_nothing`, worker 8 seals its own record naming worker 7's token, so fencing, not the seal, refuses it.

## Follow-ups from #571's delta review
- **The restore check also refuses held and blocked keys** (`crates/pipeline/src/lib.rs`, the start's lease restore). A saved key that was tombstoned, quarantined or put on the filter's hash floor since it was saved is restored only to end as `Blocked`, as the built-in worker's intake ends a key it refuses. It's never fetched or saved again, it isn't kept for late results, and its funnel row is terminal, so the funnel still reconciles. Test: `saved_keys_held_or_blocked_are_not_restored`.
- **The filter's hash-floor arm of the parked-key refusal is tested**: `parked_keys_keep_their_privacy_and_refusals` now parks a CS-1 key alongside the held one. Both end as blocked, and only the private and public keys reach the pool.
- **The worst case rotates a new set of rows each day**, the most the daily cap lets rotate. This is in `bench/examples/worker-rollups.rs` and in `crates/api/tests/insights.rs`'s eight-worker year. Measured as WK-9b's decisions file describes:
  - **Storage:** 8.44 MB a year (491,887 rows, 273,152 of them `BY_WORKER`), against 8.40 MB with the same rows every day. Its realistic fleet is unchanged at 6.47 MB, and the coordinator alone takes 2.97 MB. The rotating count matches the cap's arithmetic: 720 hours × 288 rows, plus 335 days × 288, plus the coordinator's rows.
  - **Pages:** Discover's "all" took 21.2–21.4 ms over three release runs; the slowest page, a compacted "all" range of the overview or Fetch, took 25.3–34.2 ms. The budget is 50 ms.
- **`FLEET_ROWS_PER_DAY`'s doc comment** quotes the measured range (21.2–23.2 ms) instead of "21–26 ms".

## Notes for later slices
- **WK-10:** share `Attached::keys` with `WorkerSnapshots::keys()` and set the wire keys at coordinator start. The worker must encode with `encode_with(&[], &sealed)`: a record in clear is now refused.
- **WK-10: a record that doesn't open ends its lease as `Invalid`,** a final loss (runner, 2026-11-07: fail closed now, WK-10 decides). **This supersedes WK-8's WK-11 note** that such a record's key "is fetched again".
  - **The cost:** an honest worker whose clock is a few seconds past UTC midnight seals under tomorrow's key, which the coordinator doesn't hold yet. That record's key is lost, not fetched again.
  - **WK-10 chooses one of two fixes:**
    - accept tomorrow's key within the clock-skew tolerance (60 s); or
    - requeue a record refused with `Error::Invalid("wire key")`, ending its lease as a loss the key is fetched again after, instead of reporting `Invalid`. A seal that fails under a known key stays `Invalid`, as does an inside that doesn't match its header.
- **WK-13:** `remote_records_total{outcome="clear"}` and `{outcome="unopened"}` per worker are quarantine signals. Today they're fleet-wide counters; a per-worker count belongs with WK-13's standing.
