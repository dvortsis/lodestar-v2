# SRC-16 decisions (Jobs view)

- **A Settings screen, not a route.** The Jobs view is `features/jobs/screen.ts` in the Crawling group, ordered after Workers (runner, plan Q17). One line is added to `SCREEN_ORDER` in `settings/shell/screens.ts`. Sources and import progress don't exist yet, so the links to Jobs from them are left to the slices that add those screens.
- **Server-reported state only.** Pause, resume and cancel send the request, and the row then becomes the `BackgroundJob` the server returned. Nothing is changed first. A refused control shows the server's message, and the list is refetched. A `cancelled` job without `finished_at` is the server accepting a cancel its worker hasn't reached, so it is shown as "Cancelling" with no controls (§9).
- **Polling.** `GET /jobs` every 2 s while a job is unfinished, every 10 s otherwise. A failed refresh keeps the rows and says they may be out of date. A list fetched before a control's answer is dropped.
- **Progress** counts `done + skipped` against `total`, as the server's `total` includes skipped items. An unknown total shows an indeterminate bar. An estimated total shows "about". Rate and time left show only while running.
- **Client-derived progress (flagged under §2.19).** `/jobs` has no read count or ratio, so the screen shows `done + skipped` of `total` and a percentage floored to a whole number (never 100% before the end). Both come from fields the server reports about one job, not from cohorts or counts across data; an estimated total (`total_approx`) is marked ≈. A server-side `read` or `progress` field would remove this, if the owner prefers.
- **Hidden tab.** Polling pauses while the tab is hidden and refreshes when it shows again.
- **Focus.** After a control, focus moves to the row's first remaining control, or its heading, since the pressed button is replaced.
- **Size.** The registry entry (title in en and zh, the lazy import, one `SCREEN_ORDER` line) is the expected cost of a screen. The runner accepted +0.17 kB on "Settings section", which stays under its 45 kB limit. No existing limit changed. "Settings JobsScreen (first view)" gets a 28.5 KB limit (measured 27.57 KB, +3.4%) instead of the 45 KB of the other hand-built screens. `/t/[hash]` did not move.
