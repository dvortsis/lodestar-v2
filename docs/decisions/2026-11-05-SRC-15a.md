# SRC-15a decisions

Settings → Crawling → Sources (`web/src/lib/features/sources/**`), from `GET /sources` and `PATCH /sources/{id}` (SRC-14). Spec: `docs/plan/2026-11-01-v06-sources.md` §4, architecture §18.8, §10.10, §10.14.

- **The yield "sparkline" is a bar, not a line.** `GET /sources` returns two 24 h counts (`harvested_24h`, `stored_24h`) and no time series, so a line has nothing to draw. The row shows a tiny in-house SVG bar (stored out of found) with both counts beside it, written through `formatCount`. The client shows no percentage (standards §4). A series, if wanted, is an API change for a later slice.
- **Grouping is by a source's first listed kind**, in the order discovery, importer, peer finder, enrichment, network, output; a source with no known kind goes in "Other". A source with two kinds appears once.
- **A source that still needs setup has its enable toggle disabled and sends nothing.** "Needs setup" is derived from the API's `needs`, `consent_required`, `password_required` and stored credentials. The server's consent gate (§18.7) stays the only judge: when the row says nothing is needed, the PATCH is still checked there, and a refusal is shown in plain words and the list reloaded. Consent dialogs, credentials, the password prompt and purge are SRC-15b. The row says setup is "not available on this screen yet" until then.
- **Turning a source off, and changing priority, send a PATCH at once** and the reply replaces the row. A change to on/off shows "Applies at next start" from `restart_required`.
- **Shared files touched** (one line each, as the blocklists slice did): `.size-limit.mjs` (`bodyMaps.SourcesScreen: []`; Sources reads no settings section) and the two shell tests that list the navigation (`screens.test.ts`, `SettingsShell.test.ts`), which gain "Sources" after "Network & VPN".
- **Size budget (runner §2.26 decision: trim, raise no limit).** Two shared growths came from the first build and were removed: the `minus-circle` icon, which `FacetRail` also uses, moved into a new shared chunk (so the Sources pill inlines its small "off" icon instead), and `sources/body.ts` was counted in every Settings first view (it now sits in `.size-limit.mjs`'s `securityLoader` list with the other bodies' forwarders, as Blocklists' does). What is left in the shared chunks is the registry entry itself, +58 B gzip. `main` had only 43 B of room under the 45 kB cap, so it still tips Settings section (+16 B), FiltersScreen (+56 B) and NotificationsScreen (+59 B) over; those go back to the runner.
- **Sources has its own budget line:** "Settings SourcesScreen (first view)", 30 KB for a measured 29.24 KB.
- **Trim first, then +64 B (runner §2.26, 2026-11-05).** The runner asked for ~60 B to be freed in the shared Settings chunk before any limit moved. I looked at that chunk (the registry, `screens.ts`, built as one file) and found nothing to cut without a behaviour change: it holds every screen's title in en and zh, the lazy-load preload lists and the gear icon that `NavIcon` and the General screen share; tree-shaking has already dropped the unused exports. Sources' own share is its title message, its entry and one preload list, +58 B gzip. So three limits rise by the measured residue rounded up to 64 B: Settings section 45 → 45.07 kB (residue 16 B), FiltersScreen 31 → 31.07 kB (56 B) and NotificationsScreen 45 → 45.07 kB (59 B). The owner's 45 kB route cap (§10.1) is therefore exceeded by 70 B on those two views; the runner flags it to the owner.
