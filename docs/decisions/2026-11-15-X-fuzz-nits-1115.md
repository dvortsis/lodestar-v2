# X-fuzz-nits-1115 decisions (#941)

The optional fuzz findings from the reviews of #918 and #935, collected by the runner (2026-11-15). This file says only what changed. No oracle or assertion changed.

- **`peer_fetch`: what it doesn't reach, in its doc comment and the README.**
  - The pace checks (`Error::Behind`) and the first-window deadline (`Error::FirstWindow`) fall due seconds into a fetch: `PACE_FREE` and `FIRST_WINDOW_DEADLINE` are 2 s and 4 s.
  - The target runs the whole fetch in one poll, in microseconds, on a runtime that is never driven, so no check ever falls due.
  - Their unit tests in `lodestar_peer_wire::fetch` cover them (`a_peer_under_half_pace_is_behind_at_the_next_check`, `a_first_window_that_stops_ends_when_due`, and others).
  - The docs-only option, as the runner asked: no paused clock and no tokio `test-util` in the fuzz crate.
- **`mse`: formatted with rustfmt, and `chunks_exact(4)` became `as_chunks::<4>()`.**
  - `slice::as_chunks` is stable since Rust 1.88, and the fuzz crate builds on the current nightly.
  - It yields the same four-byte ops and drops the same remainder, so behaviour is unchanged.
  - A 30 s run from its seeds found nothing.
- **README, deep runs:** the `PROPTEST_CASES` paragraph now names the checks that keep their own count, because they build their own `TestRunner` or draw a fixed number of values:
  - `the_property_reads_trails` (`crates/filter/tests/props.rs`);
  - `most_generated_queries_parse` (`crates/search/tests/query_props.rs`);
  - `generators_produce_valid_values` (`crates/companions/src/inputs.rs`).
