# GS-2 decisions (grouping producer: `group_key`, `group_minhash`; siblings' similar payloads)

Spec: `docs/plan/2026-11-05-v07-m7.md` §3, §4.3 "GS-2", Q8–Q11; architecture §6 "Grouping/dedup", §8, §3; the population contract (`docs/decisions/2026-11-04-SB-6.md`); GS-0's producer pattern (`2026-11-12-GS-0.md`); GS-1's groups and B13's withheld rule (`2026-11-12-GS-1.md`).

- **`group_key` stays unproduced (generation 0); the runner's call under §2.26 and Q10, flagged to the owner (§2.19).** Q8 makes it `title_identity`'s value in the fast column. But `schema.rs` declares it `INDEXED | FAST`, so writing it also copies `title_identity`'s whole term dictionary: measured 29.9 B per torrent (terms 18.5, fast 8.0, postings 2.4, norms 1.0), of which only the 8 B column is new. That alone leaves ~5 B under 512 B for MinHash, RX-1 and ENR-11. **GS-3's options, for it to settle with the runner:** (1) a FAST-only `group_key` (~9 B), which is a schema option change: Tantivy won't open the old schema, so it needs a `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump and a rebuild, best at a release boundary; or (2) collapse on `title_identity`'s existing terms (no new bytes, but no per-document column: the group of each hit in the phase-1 window is read another way). Nothing reads `group_key` today, and it reports `NoProducer`, so no consumer can take its absence for "no group".
- **`group_minhash` (Q9, with Q10 (a)), in the new `lodestar_classify::group`** so search and the API read one definition:
  - **Major files:** content (non-padding) files of at least 1 MiB **and** at least 1 % of the payload. So a torrent has at most 100 of them, and the work is bounded whatever its file count (6 hashes × ≤ 100 sizes).
  - **Threshold:** major files of at least 3 **distinct** sizes, or no signature (`Unsigned::FewMajorFiles`); those torrents rely on `payload_sig`. **Refinement (§2.19), from the fresh review:** Q9 says "≥ 3 major files". Unrelated split archives share their volume size, though: scene `.rNN` parts of 50,000,000 bytes, `.7z.001` volumes, 4 GiB FAT32 splits. Two different films of 19 such parts each would read as Jaccard 19/21 ≈ 0.9, and CS-8a's specificity rule doesn't stop them (any payload of 50 MiB or more is specific). So equal sizes count once for the threshold, and two similar torrents must also **share** at least 3 distinct sizes (`group::similar`). The multiset still decides the Jaccard, so a season of equal-sized episodes plus distinct extras still matches its own variants.
  - **Signature:** MinHash over the major sizes as a **multiset**. An element is `(size, k)`, `k` counting earlier copies of the same size, so three equal episodes count three times, and the result is independent of file order. `crates/classify/tests/group.rs` adds a split-archive corpus (three volume sizes, 10–50 parts, random last parts): never similar. The hashes are `fmix64` with fixed seeds (as `payload`), pinned by goldens.
  - **2 bands of 3 (6 hashes), not Q9's 4 of 4** (Q10 (a), the runner's call). 4 × 4 measured 40.0 B per torrent (each band a nearly unique u64 term; ~45 % of the synthetic corpus has 3 or more major files) and the torrent index at 545.6 B, over 512. 2 × 3 measured 19.5 B (terms 17.3, postings 2.2). **2 × 3 rather than Q10's 2 × 4:** the same term cost, more found at the threshold (theory 0.76 against 0.65 at Jaccard 0.8, 0.93 against 0.88 at 0.9), at the price of more candidates at Jaccard 0.5 (0.23 against 0.12). Every candidate is confirmed exactly, so the extra candidates cost lookups, never wrong answers.
  - **Band terms:** each band's 3 values hashed with its band's position, so equal values in different bands are different terms. Written to `group_minhash` (u64, indexed, several).
  - **Similar:** both signed, at least 3 distinct sizes shared, and the **exact** multiset Jaccard similarity of the two torrents' major sizes is at least 4/5, computed on the stored file trees (`group::similar`). The plan reads "confirmed by the estimated Jaccard ≥ the threshold on the stored sizes". **Refinement (§2.19): confirmation uses the exact Jaccard of the stored sizes, not the MinHash estimate.** The estimate is what the band terms already are; the exact value is cheap (≤ 100 sizes each) and means a candidate found by chance never shows.
  - **Rates** (`crates/classify/tests/group.rs`, a fixed synthetic corpus; theory `1 − (1 − J³)²`): Jaccard 0.8 found 74 % (theory 76 %), 0.9 found 94 % (93 %); candidates at Jaccard 0.5: 23 % (23 %), at 0.3: 7 % (5 %). No pair of 3,000 unrelated payloads shares a band. The bounds the test holds: ≥ 65 % found at 0.8, ≥ 85 % at 0.9, ≤ 35 % candidates at 0.5, ≤ 10 % at 0.3, 0 unrelated pairs, and nothing below 0.8 confirmed. **Known limit:** about a quarter of the similar payloads at exactly 0.8 aren't found; recall rises quickly above it. Siblings says nothing about this: a missing similar torrent is a miss, never a false match.
- **Population (SB-6's contract, as GS-0).** `GENERATIONS["group_minhash"]` is now 1. An existing index walks its torrents once and reports the field `Backfilling` until the walk completes. A blue/green rebuild writes it for every torrent; new documents carry it. `crates/search/tests/grouping.rs` plays GS-0's build → this build with a restart mid-walk. **Test stand-ins:** tests that used `group_minhash` as "a field with no producer" now use `name_trigram` (`index.rs`, `tests/index.rs`; a workspace grep confirms nothing writes `name_trigram`: only its schema, analyzer and RX-0's plan name it), and the v5 → v7 rebuild test counts `group_minhash` as produced (`tests/rebuild.rs`), as GS-0 did for `payload_sig`.
- **Siblings gains `similar_payload` and `similar_payload_partial`** (`/torrents/{hash}/siblings`):
  - **Reference rules, in order:** no content files → `no_content_files`; a partial stored list → `partial_file_list`; a payload that isn't specific under CS-8a's rule → `payload_not_specific` (as identical payload: a payload of round or tiny sizes is shared by unrelated torrents, and similarity on it means nothing either); major files of fewer than 3 distinct sizes → the new **`few_major_files`**; search off → `search_disabled`; an index without the field → the new **`needs_group_minhash`** (`Availability::Missing`, an older index serving during a rebuild). **A torrent without a signature never lists the other signature-less ones.**
  - **Candidates:** the new `SearchIndex::torrents_with_minhash_bands` (shared edit in `index.rs`, approved by the runner; the caller passes the 2 band terms) ORs the reference's band terms, applies the request's visibility clauses (`discover::shown(Node::All, adult)`) before the window, and returns the newest 1,000 (`SIMILAR_CANDIDATES`) under the request's `CancelToken`, as GS-1's `torrents_with_pieces_roots` does. Withheld torrents never fill the window.
  - **Confirmation:** each candidate goes through GS-1's `confirmed`: still stored, not withheld by the stored state (`discover::withheld`, hidden always, Adult unless shown) whatever the index says, its stored list not partial, and its stored sizes similar (exact Jaccard ≥ 4/5). **Identical clones are left out** (equal sorted content sizes): `identical_payload` lists them.
  - **Order, cap, partial:** newest first, at most `MAX_SIBLINGS` (20), `truncated` when more pass, or when the list is full and the window was bounded (a short list never hints at torrents it couldn't show, GS-1's rule). `similar_payload_partial` while `group_minhash` isn't Ready.
  - **Copy:** two message pairs (en, zh) for the new reasons in `discover/messages`, and their cases in `unavailable.ts`.
- **GS-1 follow-ups (rv-772 O2):** endpoint tests for the `Missing` paths of identical payload (`needs_payload_signature`), the same file (`needs_piece_roots`) and similar payloads (`needs_group_minhash`). The fixture writes the index again under an older version's directory without those fields and serves it through `Serving` with its rebuild held, as an older index serves during a rebuild. That needs Tantivy in `crates/api`'s dev-dependencies (`=0.27.0`, search's version and features; the runner approved it; `Cargo.lock` gains only the edge).
- **Size (`size-report --torrents 50000`):** the torrent index goes from 476.6 B (GS-0) to **496.0 B per torrent (+19.4 B)**, PASS under §3's 512 B: `group_minhash` terms 17.3 B, postings 2.2 B. The other budgets are unchanged (store 948.3 ≤ 1024, file index 789.2 ≤ 800, file-tree blob 346.1 ≤ 600). The two measurements that led here: 4 × 4 bands with `group_key` written, 545.6 B (FAIL); 2 × 3 bands with `group_key` written, 525.6 B (FAIL). **Headroom left under 512 B for RX-1 and ENR-11 together: 16.0 B** (the runner's number, plan Q10). `group_key`'s 1.0 B of norms was there before GS-2 (SB-6's `INDEXED` option), as `payload_sig`'s was before GS-0.
