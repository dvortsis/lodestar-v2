# GS-2 decisions (grouping producer: `group_key`, `group_minhash`; siblings' similar payloads)

Spec: `docs/plan/2026-11-05-v07-m7.md` §3, §4.3 "GS-2", Q8–Q11; architecture §6 "Grouping/dedup", §8, §3; the population contract (`docs/decisions/2026-11-04-SB-6.md`); GS-0's producer pattern (`2026-11-12-GS-0.md`); GS-1's groups and B13's withheld rule (`2026-11-12-GS-1.md`).

- **`group_key` is `title_identity`'s value in the fast column (Q8).** `TorrentDoc::to_document` writes the record's `TitleIdentityParts::key` to both fields: `title_identity` (indexed, B13b reads it) and `group_key` (indexed and fast, for GS-3's collapse). There's no second identity rule. A torrent without a parsed title with words has neither. **A change to `TitleIdentityParts::key` bumps both generations** (its doc comment and `population.rs` say so).
- **`group_minhash` (Q9), in the new `lodestar_classify::group`** so search and the API read one definition:
  - **Major files:** content (non-padding) files of at least 1 MiB **and** at least 1 % of the payload. So a torrent has at most 100 of them, and the work is bounded whatever its file count (16 hashes × ≤ 100 sizes).
  - **Threshold:** at least 3 major files, or no signature (`Unsigned::FewMajorFiles`); those torrents rely on `payload_sig`.
  - **Signature:** 16 MinHash values over the major sizes as a **multiset**. An element is `(size, k)`, `k` counting earlier copies of the same size, so three equal episodes count three times, and the result is independent of file order. The hashes are `fmix64` with fixed seeds (as `payload`), pinned by goldens.
  - **Band terms:** 4 bands of 4 values, each hashed with its band's position, so equal values in different bands are different terms. Written to `group_minhash` (u64, indexed, several).
  - **Similar:** the **exact** multiset Jaccard similarity of the two torrents' major sizes is at least 4/5, computed on the stored file trees (`group::similar`). The plan reads "confirmed by the estimated Jaccard ≥ the threshold on the stored sizes". **Refinement (§2.19): confirmation uses the exact Jaccard of the stored sizes, not the 16-value estimate.** The estimate is what the band terms already are; the exact value is cheap (≤ 100 sizes each) and means a candidate found by chance never shows.
  - **Rates** (`crates/classify/tests/group.rs`, a fixed synthetic corpus; theory `1 − (1 − J⁴)⁴`): Jaccard 0.8 found 87 % (theory 88 %), 0.9 found 99 % (99 %); candidates at Jaccard 0.5: 23 % (23 %), at 0.3: 4 %. No pair of 3,000 unrelated payloads shares a band. The stated bounds the test holds: ≥ 75 % found at 0.8, ≥ 95 % at 0.9, ≤ 35 % candidates at 0.5, ≤ 10 % at 0.3, 0 unrelated pairs, and nothing below 0.8 confirmed.
- **Population (SB-6's contract, as GS-0).** `GENERATIONS["group_key"]` and `["group_minhash"]` are now 1. An existing index walks its torrents once for both (one pass serves every field whose generation changed), and each reads `Backfilling` until the walk completes. A blue/green rebuild writes both for every torrent. New documents carry both. `crates/search/tests/grouping.rs` plays GS-0's build → this build with a restart mid-walk. **Test stand-ins:** tests that used `group_key`/`group_minhash` as "a field with no producer" now use `name_trigram` (`index.rs`, `tests/index.rs`), and the v5 → v7 rebuild test counts both as produced (`tests/rebuild.rs`), as GS-0 did for `payload_sig`.
- **Siblings gains `similar_payload` and `similar_payload_partial`** (`/torrents/{hash}/siblings`):
  - **Reference rules, in order:** no content files → `no_content_files`; a partial stored list → `partial_file_list`; a payload that isn't specific under CS-8a's rule → `payload_not_specific` (as identical payload: a payload of round or tiny sizes is shared by unrelated torrents, and similarity on it means nothing either); fewer than 3 major files → the new **`few_major_files`**; search off → `search_disabled`; an index without the field → the new **`needs_group_minhash`** (`Availability::Missing`, an older index serving during a rebuild). **A torrent without a signature never lists the other signature-less ones.**
  - **Candidates:** the new `SearchIndex::torrents_with_minhash_bands` (shared edit in `index.rs`, approved by the runner) ORs the reference's band terms, applies the request's visibility clauses (`discover::shown(Node::All, adult)`) before the window, and returns the newest 1,000 (`SIMILAR_CANDIDATES`) under the request's `CancelToken`, as GS-1's `torrents_with_pieces_roots` does. Withheld torrents never fill the window.
  - **Confirmation:** each candidate goes through GS-1's `confirmed`: still stored, not withheld by the stored state (`discover::withheld`, hidden always, Adult unless shown) whatever the index says, its stored list not partial, and its stored sizes similar (exact Jaccard ≥ 4/5). **Identical clones are left out** (equal sorted content sizes): `identical_payload` lists them.
  - **Order, cap, partial:** newest first, at most `MAX_SIBLINGS` (20), `truncated` when more pass, or when the list is full and the window was bounded (a short list never hints at torrents it couldn't show, GS-1's rule). `similar_payload_partial` while `group_minhash` isn't Ready.
  - **Copy:** two message pairs (en, zh) for the new reasons in `discover/messages`, and their cases in `unavailable.ts`.
