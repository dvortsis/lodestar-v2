## Runner rulings on the way (§2.26 performance and UX; flagged §2.19)
- **Size limits** (`web/.size-limit.mjs`, each with its comment):
  - Search index-state banners: 3.3 → 4.1 KB (FX-1). The illustrated states follow §10.19; other entries shrank by −4.8 KB in total.
  - Detail sections: 37.3 → 37.31 KB (FX-1), then 37.52 KB (FX-5). The page really widens (I1), the tree is sized to its visible rows, and the parsed rows changed (P4).
  - `/t/[hash]` route: 21.0 → 21.14 KB (FX-5, the I2 connecting glyph).
  - Settings logging messages: → 1.05 kB (FX-4: real labels for the two log-level fields, loaded on demand).
  - Settings logging parts: 17.1 → 17.15 KB. `main` went red when CI measured 3 B over a local build; the cause is build variance, not new code.
- **Noise allowance on the reporting-overhead gate** (#344). The timed deltas are gated at +8% (search p95) and −5% (ingest), which is the §3 budget plus 3 points. The duty-cycle gate (≤ 1%) is the operative one, because the paired deltas sit inside run-to-run spread. The §3 budget itself is unchanged.
- **Keyboard applies at once** (FX-3). With no Save button, a change made stale by another tab re-sends itself once, merged with the server's state. Undo inverts only its own change and is offered only once nothing later is queued.
- **The query language gains `a..<b`** (I9, #350). Its AST is the same as `a..(b−1)`. It is **not printed** until stage 2, one release after the parser ships. The reason: filter rules store printed text, and an older binary drops rows it can't parse, so printing it now could loosen filtering after a rollback (Opus security review). I9 is fixed only for the lower bound and one-sided bounds until then.
