# C-adult-other decisions

Fixes a content-safety false negative found in the R-relevance review (#94): a torrent typed Other (no known extension, a lone archive, documents) or Ebook was never called adult, even with the `XXX` tag at score 90, so it showed in default search while Adult was hidden.

- **Other and Ebook get an adult kind from detection** (§20.9 "As built"; supersedes "Ebook and Other keep their type"). At or above the threshold, Other becomes **Scene** and Ebook **Comics**, the nearest kinds an Is adult correction already gives (`forced_kind`). VR words turn Other's Scene into VR, as they do for Video and TV; Ebook stays Comics. *Why:* hiding Adult by default is the owner's rule (§1.8.12), and a full-score adult name that slips past it is a miss, not caution.
- **The threshold is the same 60 for every type.** *Why:* the signals, the counters and the season rule are the same whatever the type, and Software (the other archive-heavy type) has mapped to Game at 60 since classifier v2 with no false positive in the corpus. Every mainstream Other and Ebook control in the corpus stays out: a novel called *Porno*, *The Joy of Sex*, lecture notes with two adult terms (the counter wins), a documentary with two terms, an episodic *Sex Explained*, an `nsfw` model archive, a plain backup archive, the *xXx* soundtrack. A stricter rule for Other would have needed a strong signal and lost term-only names like `JAV.Uncensored.Pack.rar`, the same names that are adult as Video.
- **Every segment of the largest file's path is scanned, not only its name.** *Why:* a torrent named by its hash whose only file is `Some.Title.XXX.1080p/video.mp4` showed the same gap. Counters on a folder (`Documentary/…`) count the same way. The torrent name, which is the root folder, was already scanned.
- **`CLASSIFY_VERSION` is 3.** Stored torrents pick up the change through background enrichment: every torrent whose `torrent_parsed.parser_ver` isn't the new `ENRICHMENT_VERSION` is classified again, its `content_type` rewritten and a `reindex_log` entry added, so the search index follows. A torrent that becomes adult this way is judged as adult by the filter engine (§20.7), so the stricter Adult-only checks apply to it. No migration.
- **Refinement under §2.19.** This refines "detection is cautious, so mainstream titles stay out of Adult" (§1.8.12) without loosening it: no counter-signal, score or threshold changes, and mainstream controls are pinned by fixtures. It only stops a full-confidence adult signal from being dropped for two types. The runner flags it to the owner.
