# X-cr-round1-gate decisions

Round 1's judging side (`docs/plan/2026-11-27-revised-path-to-100.md` revision 6, §5.2 items 2, 5, 6, 9, 10 and 12, §6.1–§6.4, §8; owner O1–O4, 2026-11-27). The collect slice (X-cr-round1-collect) writes the files; this slice reads them. `gate` and `effect` are unchanged for historical runs (§8 item 8).

## Commands
- **`analyze_cr.py segment RUN --declaration D`** judges one round-1 comb arm. With `--declaration`:
  - **Rule 5.5 becomes the declaration** (§8 item 1): `balanced_preset` and `preset_stayed_balanced` are replaced by `declared_preset` and `preset_stayed_declared`. `declared_switches`, `declared_image_digest` and `safe_restart_on` are checked at start, and `switches_stayed_declared` wherever a boundary read carries the switches. Each is `void` on a mismatch. `effective_switches_recorded` is `missing` (invalid) without the §5.1 gauge's 13 values.
  - **The arm is found by `run.json` `round1`** (`round`, `attempt`, `day`, `arm`). No matching entry is `invalid` (`declaration_entry_found`).
  - **Fresh store** (`fresh_store_at_start`, invalid): the first scrape's `torrents` value is 0.
  - **Bundle evidence and caps** (below), and `memory`, the measured-window fit.
  - Only a comb arm takes a declaration; anything else is an error.
- **`analyze_cr.py gate-round MANIFEST`** gives every arm's verdict and the selection from one manifest (§5.2 item 12). Output fields: `candidates` and `diagnostics` (per arm: `crawler`, `memory`, `shipping_eligible`, `reasons`, `days[]` with `status`, `reasons`, `tests`, `memory`, `contention`, `solo_confirmation_required`), `selection` (`ships`, `state` = `ships` | `pending_retry` | `round1_report`, `waiting_on`, `trail`), `inputs` (every path's SHA-256) and `manifest_sha256`. It's deterministic; a test runs it twice and compares bytes.

A malformed declaration is an error (exit 2), not a verdict: it's frozen before the rehearsal, so it's fixed, not judged.

## Draft collector schema (the interface with X-cr-round1-collect; replace with theirs when it arrives)

The runner sets `diagnosis` after its diagnosis with a fresh reviewer (§6.3); any other class or diagnosis is `unknown_event` (not judged).
- **Host samples** (one per day): `interval_seconds` = 10, `backing_device`, `rehearsal_rtt_median_ms` {arm: ms}, `samples` [{`utc`, `cpu` (busy share of all 8 vCPU, 0–1), `steal` (0–1), `disk_write_ms` (null with no completed write), `disk_inflight`, `uplink_drops_total` (cumulative), `tunnels` {arm: {`rtt_ms`, `lost`}}}].
- **Manifest:** `schema` 1, `kind` `cr_round1_manifest`, `declaration`, `comparator`, `days[]` {`attempt`, `day`, `repeat`, `reports` {arm: ref}, `archive`, `contention`}; each ref is {`path` (relative, inside the archive), `sha256`}.

## §6.1's evidence table, as coded (step-0 note 2026-11-27; the runner accepted the new rows and freezes them in the CR-SPEC round-1 decisions file)
- **Existing checks, run whenever the switch is on, whatever the run kind:** coordinated admission and uTP (rules 5.1–5.2, `mechanism`); `lookup_end` runs the lookup-cost pair (`lookup_cost_pair`: ended on values, slots above 32 once, query rate); `hint_first` (`hint_first`); `sampler_cooldown_split` (`sampler_split`; a table that never filled is reported `not_exercised`; its series absent is void); `soft_timeout` (the counter rendered; 0 is a result).

## `gate-round`'s rules, as coded
- **Per candidate, its two days pair within its latest attempt** (§6.3). Earlier attempts' days are still judged and listed in `earlier_attempts`.
- **A valid miss stands wherever it is** (§6.2, §6.4: a replicate, not a second chance): a judged `fail` in an earlier attempt, or in a day a repeat followed, makes the candidate `fail` (listed in `valid_misses`), even while a day is still missing.
- **A repeated day (`repeat` > 0) replaces the day it repeats only if that day wasn't judged** (§6.3 repeats a day after an evidenced fault). Repeating a passing day is `repeat_of_a_judged_day`, and repeating one not judged for any other reason (contention, a product failure, a void or invalid report) is `repeat_without_infrastructure_fault`; both are not judged. Only an evidenced infrastructure fault repeats a day: contention calls for a solo confirmation, a product failure for a diagnosis.
- **Retry budget:** more than two repeated days in the round, in any layout, is `retry_budget_exhausted`. A solo confirmation's own two days are a fresh attempt at `repeat` 0, so they never count.
- **Candidate-level problems are `not_judged`** (not authorized, a comparator problem, spacing or an exhausted budget even over a valid miss; the rest yield to a valid miss): not authorized (diagnostic), the attempt doesn't declare days 1 and 2, a day missing, a comparator problem (missing, unbooked, not `kind=comparator`, not `valid`, not a 24 h non-rehearsal window, another host or network identity, or `ended` before `timeline.window_end_utc`), duplicate run IDs, overlapping windows, a repeated exit (the same label or the same hash on both days), rule 2.2 spacing against every Lodestar report in the round (from the comparator's `timeline.window_end_utc`).
- **Day-level, any problem makes that day `not_judged`:** the layout isn't the attempt's (exits exactly as §6.3's table; S is one arm on two different exits), the report isn't a valid 24 h comb window or isn't bound to its declaration (placement, spec commit, host and network identity, run ID, image digest, fresh store), the roster differs from the declaration, a peer's report is missing or not bound to its own placement and run ID, two reports share a run ID, an arm wasn't active from the candidate's `ws` through its final boundary, a leak test didn't pass, an egress reading is missing at a boundary (± 90 s) or differs from the arm's exit, two exits share a hash, an infrastructure fault on the candidate or on any peer (`peer_infrastructure_fault`: the whole concurrent day is repeated), a product failure on any arm, or contention isn't `ok`.
- **Verdict:** any candidate-level problem → `not_judged`; else a judged day that misses → `fail` (a valid miss ends the claim, §6.4); both days judged and passing → `pass`; otherwise `not_judged`.
- **Memory:** `pass` only with both days' `meets_bar` true; `fail` if either is false; otherwise `not_judged`. Shipping eligibility is crawler `pass` and memory `pass`.
- **Selection (§6.2):** in `selection_order`, a diagnostic arm only selects; the first authorized candidate whose disposition isn't terminal stops the selection at `pending_retry`; the first shipping-eligible one ships; otherwise `round1_report`. Terminal dispositions: shipping-eligible, crawler-accepted with memory failing, a valid miss, a product failure diagnosed as one (`diagnosis: product`; pending is incomplete, so A isn't read), out of retries (budget, or a failed solo confirmation).

## Contention (§6.3), thresholds as proposed, frozen by the runner
- CPU p95 ≤ 0.80 of 8 vCPU, steal p95 ≤ 0.05, `/data` write latency p99 ≤ 50 ms; per window hour, the candidate's tunnel loss ≤ 1 %, RTT p95 ≤ 2 × its rehearsal median, no uplink drops. Nearest-rank over every 10 s interval of `[ws, window end)`; a final partial interval counts, and joins hour 23 for the hourly network rule.
- **Coverage:** each series (CPU, steal, disk, the candidate's tunnel, uplink) below 98 % of intervals is `not_judged`. A missing interval ranks over every value. A disk interval with no completed write is excluded with nothing in flight and counts over with writes in flight.
- **A breach** makes the day `not_judged` with `solo_confirmation_required`; in layout S it's `solo_confirmation_failed` instead (terminal).

## Readings of open rules, for the runner (not decided as owner questions)
1. **Uplink drops are a cumulative counter, read per hour as a delta.** §6.3 says a missing interval counts over the threshold; for a "no drops" rule that would make any lost sample a breach. A counter can't hide drops in a gap, so the delta is used.
2. **A missing tunnel probe counts as lost** (and its RTT ranks over), per "a missing interval counts as over the threshold".
3. **Peers must be active from each candidate's `ws`,** literally (§5.2 item 11). Arms that start minutes apart pass, since `ws` is `T_open + 60 min`.
4. **`lookup_end` on runs the whole lookup-cost pair,** including slots above 32 once. With `preset_slots` off that can't pass; every declared arm has the two together.
5. **A candidate in several attempts pairs its days in its latest one;** a valid miss in an earlier one still ends its claim. **Runner's call (2026-11-27, flagged to the owner under §2.19):** a fresh attempt after a layout change doesn't count against the retry budget, since §6.3 counts repeated days and a fresh attempt isn't one. Layouts only shrink (L3 → L2 or L2b → S), so the declaration check refuses a later attempt that doesn't drop an arm from the one before it; a solo confirmation (S, one arm of the attempt before it) sits outside that chain. The round can't loop through fresh attempts.
9. **`switches_stayed_declared` reads the switches only where a boundary row carries them.** The §5.1 gauge is set once at start and never changes while the process runs (runner, 2026-11-27), and a restart fails rule 6.5's identity check, so the start read is the binding one.
10. **A peer's own report must be bound** (placement and run ID), but its verdict isn't required: a peer whose window voids on a mechanism check still ran its layout. A peer's product failure breaks the layout through its event.
6. **A failed solo confirmation** is reported as `not_judged` with `solo_confirmation_failed` and a terminal `out_of_retries` disposition (§6.3: "recorded as failed and the round-1 report follows"), not as `fail`: it isn't a valid miss.
7. **The collector's own bound check** (now in X-cr-round1-collect's brief) (`collect.py`, about line 2016) treats `dht_outstanding` > 1024 as a bad bound. On an Aggressive arm (cap 4096) that would trip; it's the collect slice's file.
8. **(Now in X-cr-round1-collect's brief.)** `dht_hints_captured_total`, `fetch_stream_batches_total`, `dht_target_prefix_queries_total{kind}` and `dht_sample_cooling_reason{reason}`** aren't in the collector's allowlist today, so a real snapshot drops them and the rows void. The collect slice adds them.
11. **Cross-day binding problems don't stop a valid miss.** `duplicate_run`, overlapping windows, a repeated exit and a missing day concern pairing two days; a miss on a day that is itself fully bound and judged still ends the claim. Not authorized, a comparator problem, spacing and an exhausted budget do stop it.

## From the runner's review of #1147 (2026-11-27)
- **History across attempts:** a contention breach outside layout S needs a later solo confirmation (`solo_confirmation_required`); a failed solo confirmation has no further retry (`solo_confirmation_failed`, terminal `out_of_retries`); a product failure on the same candidate digest, diagnosed or pending, withholds qualification (`product_failure_on_this_digest`; a fix makes a new candidate with a new digest). `disposition` reads product failure and out-of-retries before shipping eligibility.
- **Manifest completeness:** an attempt the declaration doesn't name is an error. Each (attempt, day)'s repeats run 0..k (`repeats_not_contiguous`), and a day listed only as repeat k counts k repeats against the budget. Every earlier attempt's declared days are listed, or the manifest records its end (`attempt_ends` {attempt: {`reason`, `days_run`}}), else `earlier_attempt_incomplete`.
- **Spacing order (§6.3):** every Lodestar process start and window start is at or after the comparator's `timeline.window_end_utc` + 12 h (`lodestar_before_comparator_end_plus_12h`), beside rule 2.2's checks.
- **Run IDs** are unique across the manifest, the comparator's included (`run_id_reused`).
- **§6.1's arm table** is part of the declaration check: B candidate, Balanced, 11 on and `route_backoff` off; A the same switches, Aggressive, candidate or diagnostic; C diagnostic, Balanced, all 12 off. No other arm.
- **Uplink drops** are the sum of positive steps; any decrease is a reset, and contention is `not_judged` (`uplink_counter_reset`).
- **`analyzer_sha256`** (this file's SHA-256) is in the verdict and in each round-1 segment report's `round1`.
- **One image for the round (§6.3):** every entry's `image_digest` and every attempt's `candidate_digests` value equal the declaration's `image_digest`, or the declaration is refused (re-review of 970a6556: a wrong digest declared for an earlier attempt once hid its valid miss and its product failure).
- **`attempt_ends`:** `days_run` is exactly `[]`, `[1]` or `[1, 2]` (plain integers), and each day it names is listed in the manifest. **Runner's ruling:** an earlier attempt ended with fewer than its declared days hides history, so the candidate is `not_judged` (`earlier_attempt_history_unseen`) unless that attempt has days to see and every one passed with no reason; with `days_run: []` there is nothing to see, so it's `not_judged`. `attempt_ends` is copied into the verdict, at the top level and per candidate.
