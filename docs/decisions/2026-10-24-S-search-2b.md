# S-search-2b decisions (the facet rail)

The search page's facet rail (architecture §10.4, §10.15), on top of 2a's results list (`2026-10-24-S-search-2a.md`). Items marked **(§2.19)** refine an owner decision and are for the runner to flag.

## Counts and clicks

- **Counts are the server's, on the rows' snapshot.**
  - `POST /facets` takes the shown query (`results.shown`) with `lease` set to the results' first `next_cursor`, a new `results.lease`.
  - The rail never computes a count; "≈" shows only on `approx`, with one sampled note per panel (the shared `sampled_note`).
- **A click is an AST edit** (`facets/toggle.ts`), handed to W5 after `ensureSearchPath()`, so each one is a single history entry and Back undoes it.
  - It recognises a facet's own clauses by the server's rule (`Facet::owns`), so selection and the disjunctive counts always agree.
  - It rewrites them into at most two clauses, the selected values and a negation of the excluded ones, in place of the first.
  - It leaves mixed clauses (`res:1080p | type:movie`) alone, as the server does.
  - It rewrites only own clauses whose meaning survives: a leaf, an OR of leaves, or the negation of either. Others (`-(res:a & res:b)`, `res:a | -res:b`) stay as they are, since turning them into "selected" and "excluded" lists would change the query's logic. A click then adds its own clause next to them.
  - Brushes and presets replace the field's top-level range or date clause.
  - Tests cover:
    - every state of three values against every toggle;
    - 300 seeded random queries, mixing own, mixed, nested and free-text clauses, checking that what may not be rewritten stays untouched and in order;
    - every brush round trip.
- **A yes/no flag has one value at a time:** selecting the other replaces it, and its rows have no exclude button.
- **A selected value shows four ways:** a checked box, medium weight, the value text, and its chip in the strip (W5's).

## Groups

- **The order is §10.4's:** Type, Discovered, Size, Extension, Resolution, Seeders, Trackers reporting. "More filters" (a `<details>`) has Language, Release group, Codec, Audio, HDR, Private, v2, Has, Ambiguous, Imported, Backfill, and Adult when the server lists it.
- **Histograms reuse `charts/Histogram.svelte`** (no uPlot):
  - explicit bins, empty bins drawn empty, an accessible summary;
  - a brush by pointer and keys (Enter selects all, the arrows move an edge);
  - a settled brush becomes an inclusive range: bins are `[lo, hi)`, and an edge bin left open means no bound.
- **A brush at the histogram's end keeps the clause's bound when the clause cut the histogram there.** Discovered's bins span only the current matches (histograms aren't disjunctive), so after brushing Jan 1–10 the end bins are the clause's edges. Moving one edge must not drop the other. An edge dragged out to an end, or an open top bin, is unbounded.
- **Discovered:**
  - The presets are "newer than" (`discovered:<24h`, `{ago}`).
  - A brush is a span of whole UTC days. The query language's dates are days and the server prints a date clause as days, so a sub-day edge (the server bins by hour under 48 h) wouldn't survive the canonical text in the URL.
  - "Custom" focuses the histogram to start a brush and shows how, in text (never hover-only).
  - The backfill note shows when a bin is `backfill`.
- **(§2.19) Size's open top edge reads "—".** The server sends no upper edge for its last bin.
- **Extension:** the top 8, "N in other values", and "Show more", which lists up to 100 (`limits.ext`) with a search box over them. Kept per facet across queries.
- **(§2.19) Seeders and Trackers reporting:**
  - The buckets are null until M4: "—", "Not available yet" and why, never zeros.
  - Once they arrive, the presets ≥ 1 / ≥ 5 / ≥ 50 are ranges with the server's counts.
  - "Include never-scraped" waits for a query clause that can express it (follow-up).
- **Release year** (`histograms.year`, bins `[y, y + 1)`) is a range under More filters.
- **A facet with no values for this query says nothing.** The server lists every facet it serves, values or not.
- **A group the index can't serve** (`unavailable`, e.g. during a rebuild), histograms included, says so instead of disappearing.
- **Category** (`cat:` shares) is served but not a rail group: its clause is a share range, not a value toggle (follow-up).
- **Exclusion:** the minus button keeps the name "Exclude {value}", with its state in `aria-pressed`, and an excluded value's checkbox name ends "(excluded)".
- **Junk is not shown:** the server doesn't serve it yet (follow-up).

## Layout and loading (§10.15)

- **Breakpoints (§10.15):**
  - x-large and up: the 264 px rail in flow, an `aside` in `#search-rail` with a visually hidden "Filters" heading;
  - large (640–1023 px): "Filters (N)" opens a left sheet that applies each click at once;
  - medium and small (below 640 px): a bottom sheet that stages its clicks.
  - The layout is read at once (no first render in the wrong layout), and an open sheet closes when the page crosses a breakpoint.
- **The bottom sheet stages.**
  - "Show N results" counts the staged query with its own `/facets` request (no lease: it isn't a shown query), and is said politely once it settles (§10.13).
  - "≈" marks an estimated count, as everywhere else.
  - Until the staged query differs from the shown one, the live counts serve and no second request goes out.
  - Nothing is written until Show, which applies everything as one history entry. Closing without Show writes nothing, and reopening starts from the shown query.
- **A pasted link shows the Filters button from the start** (below x-large), so it can't move the rows when they arrive. The e2e specs measure CLS at 390 and 800 px.
- **An expired or unfitting lease** (`cursor_expired`, `bad_cursor`: 10 minutes idle, or a setting changed) falls back to the index serving now, rather than failing on every Retry.
- **The host loads with the results; the rest loads where it shows.** The rail's chunk loads at x-large, or on the first Filters press below that. The counts are fetched only while the rail shows. The sheet primitive loads on demand.
- **Its styles are a plain sheet** (`facets.css`, tokens only), as 2a's are. Global CSS is unchanged at 19.83 KB.

## Sizes (`npx size-limit`)

- **"Facet rail (lazy)": 15.28 KB, over the brief's 15 KB cap after the review's fixes (reported to the runner; see the PR).** First measured 14.63 KB, measured beyond the engaged view and the results panel it loads with. The FacetRail chunk is 9.5 KB of it (the histogram included) and the host 2.8 KB.
  - Measured + 10% would be 16.1 KB, over the brief's 15 KB cap, so the cap is the limit, with 0.37 KB of headroom (reported to the runner).
  - The sheet loads on demand.
- **`/search` first load: 40.77 KB (+60 B)**, the route's import of the host. The bare page's total is unchanged within the lhci budget.
