Guided and detected companions, end to end (plan `docs/plan/2026-11-06-v06-companions.md` CMP-5g, Q7, Q12, Q19; architecture §18.7, §18.10; `docs/owner-decisions.md` §1.7.2). These are the slice's decisions under §2.33. None loosens an owner decision or a security decision. The runner flags them to the owner (§2.19).

## Split (runner, 2026-11-09)
The slice was estimated at 3.5–4.5k changed lines, so it lands as three PRs in sequence, each opened once the previous one merges:
- **CMP-5g-1:** wiring and watch. It covers CMP-6's carry-overs, the guided records' format, the watch loop, the companion gate's feed and CMP-8b's `ConfigSink`.
- **CMP-5g-2:** the guided endpoints. Add a detected companion or a URL, paste a key or temporary password, see the plan, then confirm, apply and hand over.
- **CMP-5g-3:** starting managed companions at serve. It covers `Supervised` behind `manager::monitored`, Q18's `companions.<id>.egress` and real restart, logs and regenerate. Until it merges, the managed routes keep answering `not_wired`.

## CMP-5g-1

### CMP-6's carry-overs
1. **`Service::installed` reads the journal.** It's `state::load(<data>/companions/<id>)` with an installed version, read-only (runner-approved). It no longer uses the snapshot, which detected companions now join. Inside a container it's always false. Tested: a journal that recovery hasn't published counts, and a snapshot whose journal is gone doesn't.
2. **The provenance marker** is defined here (`guided::Provenance`, its row below). CMP-5g-2 writes it when you paste a credential. The reveal will require `generated` once it does.
3. **The template data guard's positive path is tested.** On a native install, the resolved data folder is in the generator's input. Inside a container it isn't.
4. **`manager::field_of` is public** (doc only, no behaviour change), and the binary's copy is gone.
5. **I1 through the real generators:** no catalogue pin lands in this slice, so the field naming stays tested with a fake generator (`blame`). The first pinned image adds the end-to-end case.

### Guided records: the on-disk format (runner-approved, 2026-11-09)
These are `settings` rows. There's no migration, and nothing goes under `<data>/companions/<id>/`, which CMP-5's recovery treats as Lodestar's own (`crates/companions/src/guided.rs`).
- **`companions.<id>.target`:** JSON of a closed type:
  - `{"kind":"loopback","port":N}`;
  - `{"kind":"container","port":N}`;
  - `{"kind":"url","url":"…"}`.

  It's read with `deny_unknown_fields`, and at most 4 KiB.
  - A loopback or container target must be one of `detect::probe_targets` for the entry. That means one of its internal TCP ports, and for a container target, running in a container (Q12; Docker's DNS is trusted only there).
  - A URL must parse as CMP-0's `EnteredUrl`, which refuses user names and passwords, queries, fragments and any scheme but `http` and `https`. So no credential can sit in this non-secret row.
- **`companions.<id>.origin`:** `confirmed` or `guided`.
- **`companions.<id>.provenance.<field>`:** `pasted` or `generated`. `<field>` must be `field_of(id)`; any other key reads as none. It sits outside CMP-3's `secret.` prefix, because it isn't a secret.
- **Anything unparseable, unknown or out of place reads as no record.** Nothing is guessed or repaired.
- **Not in the settings API:** these rows belong to no settings section, so `/settings`, its export and the registry's golden never list them. Their only reader is the binary's `companion_wiring::records`.

### The watch (`crates/companions/src/watch.rs`)
- **Q7's cadence:** each companion you added is checked when it's added or its target changes, then every 30 s. The check uses CMP-8a's `Health` adapter and the guarded client only, on its record's target (Q12).
- **Debounced, per Q7's "3 failures in a row":** a companion seen healthy stays healthy until three checks in a row fail. That's because CMP-10 restarts a plugin on every flip it's sent. A first result counts at once, and a changed target starts the debounce again.
- **Reports:** `Healthy`, `Unhealthy` (it answered wrongly) or `Missing` (nothing answered). A companion without a health adapter (gluetun) isn't listed.

### The companion gate (`crates/lodestar/src/companion_wiring.rs`; CMP-10's contract)
- **`PipelineConfig::companions` is now `Some`** whenever companions are on for the run. A fixture keeps `None`.
- The gate holds `Unknown` until the feed's first snapshot. That's sent once the sources' ops exist, so gated plugins wait until then: it fails closed.
- **What it sends:**
  - every companion Lodestar installed, healthy only when its last check passed. Not checked yet counts as unhealthy, which is every installed companion until CMP-5g-3 starts them;
  - every companion you added, as the watch sees it, with a missing one unlisted, so its dependents show "Needs X";
  - `external_client`: an `integrations.clients` entry other than `lodestar-qbittorrent`;
  - `external_indexer`: the Prowlarr source is on, and one of the addresses it runs with isn't a companion's. Those are a record's target, or loopback 9696 for an installed Prowlarr.
- **Addresses compare canonically:** without a trailing `/`, with the scheme and host in lower case.
- **When it updates:** on any change to the installed companions, the watch, the integrations or the records, and every 30 s for the Prowlarr source being turned on or off.
- **A failed start** (the guarded client couldn't be built) is logged as `companions.install_failed`, step `wire`, and leaves the gate `Unknown`.

### `ConfigSink` (`companion_wiring::Sink`; CMP-5g-2 calls it)
- **qBittorrent:**
  - It becomes the `integrations.clients` entry `lodestar-qbittorrent` ("qBittorrent (Lodestar)"), through the settings registry's own save, so its validation and sealing apply.
  - The rest of the list is saved as it is, each other client without its password, which keeps the saved one.
  - The address is the target's, and the user name, password and category come from CMP-8b's entry.
- **Prowlarr:** the Prowlarr source gets its `api_key` credential first and then its address (`{"url": …, "manager": "prowlarr"}`), through the sources' ops. So an address never runs without its key.
- **Restarting the Prowlarr source after a hand-over (CMP-10's contract) isn't possible from here.** A saved source setting applies at Lodestar's next start, and the source shows `restart_required`, as for any saved source setting. Applying it live needs a pipeline change; that's raised with the runner.
- **Not tested here:** the sink's write through `OpsApi`. That needs a fake of the whole sources API; CMP-5g-2's confirmation tests drive it end to end. The client batch is tested.
