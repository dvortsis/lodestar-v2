# X-gs2-nits decisions (GS-2 review nits)

Follows `docs/decisions/2026-11-12-GS-2.md` (#804), from the runner's delta review `reviews/rv-804-dbf74088.md` (runner-state). Nothing here changes stored terms: `group_minhash` stays at generation 1.

- **Known limit: a common size with at most two copies can still gather on one band term** (review O1). A size kept by `signed_sizes` (at most `MAX_COPIES` = 2 copies) is still a single MinHash element that the whole class shares. With only 3 or 4 signed sizes, whether it's the minimum of all three hashes in a band depends on its own hash ranks.
  - **Who it hits:** split archives with exactly two full volumes plus a remainder and one other large file. `crates/classify/tests/group.rs` measures, over 2,000 releases each:
    - 50,000,000-byte volumes: 0.22 % of unrelated pairs share a band, and the top term carries 4.4 % of the class;
    - 4 GiB − 1 FAT32 parts: 1.2 % of pairs and 11 %.
  - **The test records bounds** of 0.5 % / 6 % and 2.5 % / 15 % (fixed seeds), so a change that makes it worse shows.
  - **Why it isn't worse:** this is a general MinHash property, a frequent element in small sets. Lowering `MAX_COPIES` to 1 wouldn't help, because a common single-copy size behaves the same way. It's a narrow tail, unlike B1's every-scene-release case. A class needs thousands of visible members to fill the 1,000-candidate window, and when it does, Siblings says `truncated`. It never claims a confident "none", and confirmation never shows a wrong match.
- **Known limit: split-archive variants are found less often** (review O4). Two releases of 20 equal volumes plus some extra large files that differ in one non-volume file are similar by the multiset Jaccard (≥ 0.9). But they become candidates only through their signed sizes, so only 24 % of the time with 2 shared extras, 39 % with 3 and 60 % with 5 (the review's simulation). That's the price of leaving the volume size out of the signature (B1). It's a miss, never a false match.
- **The copy and volume rule is pinned as stored format** (review O3). A golden with two copies of one size and three copies of another must give exactly the first golden's terms. A later change to `signed_sizes` can't silently change stored terms at generation 1.
- **`except_sig` is shown to apply before the limit** (review O2). Two more assertions in `torrents_with_minhash_bands_is_bounded_and_cancellable` give exactly the remainder with the right `more`. A filter applied after the limit would give `([4, 6], true)` and `([], true)` instead.
- **Size:** CI's `size budget (§3)` on #804's final head measured the torrent index at **495.7 B per torrent**, so **16.3 B of headroom** is left under 512 B for RX-1 and ENR-11 (review O5). This supersedes the 495.8 B / 16.2 B in GS-2's file.
