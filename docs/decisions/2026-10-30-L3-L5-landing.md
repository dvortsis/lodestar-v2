# L3–L5 landing: runner decisions

Runner, 2026-10-30, for the combined L3–L5 landing PR. Flagged to the owner under `docs/owner-decisions.md` §2.19.

## Detail drawer size entry: 28.5 KB → 29 KB

- **What changed.** L3's visibility revalidation (cached detail is never shown before a fresh check, and a content-settings change clears the detail caches) subscribes the drawer to `settings` events. That pulls the shared event client (`events.svelte`, 2.1 KB gzipped) into the drawer's own chunk set, because the search route doesn't load it yet.
- **Measured.** 28,504 B on CI and 28,492 B locally, against 28,500 B. Trims that don't change behaviour moved the figure by less than the ±15 B that chunk-hash churn adds to each build.
- **Decision.** Raise the entry to 29 KB. That is +1.7%, well under the "measured + 10%" rule the other entries use (31.3 KB). The event client leaves this entry once the shell loads it (F-status's progress-aware status pill is the expected first user).
- **Not changed.** §10.1's 15 KB drawer target and the on-demand "Detail sections" entry (33.9 KB) are unchanged. Nothing moved out of this entry into another one.
