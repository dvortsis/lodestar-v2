# B13b decisions

Siblings' "other versions" (§10.5, `docs/decisions/2026-10-22-B13.md`) for short titles with no year, season or episode, through the v7 `title_identity` term that X-search-files-5 (#505) indexed. This closes B13's **known limit**; the rest of B13's file stands.

- **The term narrows the candidates; the window stays the fallback.** Siblings compile B13's candidate query (the title phrase plus span ranges, under the same `shown` base query) and add `title_identity = key` as a required clause with a constant score of 0. The term picks only the versions of one work, however common its title, and the zero score leaves the ranking exactly as B13's query ranks. The best 1,000 candidates by relevance, the first 20 that match and `truncated` are unchanged. "Up" and "Heat" are now found behind more than 1,000 other torrents that match the title phrase and score as their versions do (the test uses the same title with a year, another work by identity, indexed first so it fills the window; `short_titles_with_no_year_find_their_versions_past_the_window`).
- **The stored records still decide.** A term hit is a candidate, not a sibling. The identity check on the stored enrichment records, and `withheld` (hidden by a filter rule; Adult while "Show adult content" is off), run on every candidate as before. A torrent reclassified since the last index commit is judged by its stored state: one reclassified out of the identity is dropped, and one reclassified into it is missed on the term path until its document is reindexed (the window could have found it). That gap is transient and bounded by the indexer's lag. Hidden and Adult versions stay withheld on both paths (`withheld_versions_stay_withheld`).
- **One identity rule.** The identity now lives in one place: `lodestar_search::TitleIdentityParts` (the folded title words plus the sorted, deduplicated year, season, episode and absolute spans) and `title_identity_parts(record)`. The index's `title_identity` hashes those parts (`TitleIdentityParts::key`, FNV-1a, unchanged from #505; the pinned value still holds). Siblings' `TitleIdentity` DTO is built from the same parts, and the query's key is computed from the DTO through the same `key`. The API no longer runs the tokenizer for this. `the_shown_identity_and_the_indexed_term_agree` checks, over a corpus of spelling variants, that the key Siblings query by is the term the indexer writes (`DocEnrichment::new`), and that two records have equal identities exactly when their terms are equal.
- **Population contract (SB-6), decided per snapshot.** The term is used only when the open index has the field and `availability("title_identity")` is `Ready`. On an index without it (before the v7 rebuild), or while a new generation of it is being backfilled, Siblings use B13's 1,000-candidate window, so a partly filled field never reads as "no versions". `a_backfilling_index_falls_back_to_the_window` builds with `Generations::with("title_identity", 2)` and shows both sides: the fallback still finds an ordinary film's versions, and it misses the "Up" versions behind the decoys, which is the limit the term fixes. The check goes through the index's own `prefilter` gate on the current snapshot, the gate every prefilter on a tracked field uses, so the term is offered only on a snapshot that sees the field complete (`the_identity_term_narrows_without_rescoring_and_waits_for_the_field`, which also shows the term leaves the scores unchanged; `an_index_without_the_identity_field_offers_no_identity_term` covers an index from before v7). Siblings don't hold a lease, so the search reads that snapshot or a newer one, and a field complete in a snapshot stays complete in every later one.
- **Shared items outside the brief's owns:** `crates/search/src/doc.rs` (`title_identity` split into `TitleIdentityParts`, `TitleIdentityParts::key` and `title_identity_parts`, with no change in behaviour); `crates/search/src/lib.rs` (their re-export); `crates/search/src/index.rs` (one accessor, `SearchIndex::query_title_identity`, whose compiled-query cache key gets an `@identity{key}` suffix; `prefilter` loses its `dead_code` expectation now that this calls it).
- **Nothing else changes:** no schema bump (v7 already carries the field), no new route, DTO field or reason key, and no change to Compare, Discover or the identity rule.
