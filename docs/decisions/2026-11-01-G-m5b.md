Projects: 360, 1024 and 1920 (dark) on every route spec; 1920 light, 360 dark, 1024 dark, `rosewood-light`, `dracula-dark`, Compact and reduced motion on smoke, states and the table, bulk and Insights specs. Neither the fixture nor the config is committed: the brief asks for the report and its screenshots only, and the harness's own snapshot policy keeps the repository small.
