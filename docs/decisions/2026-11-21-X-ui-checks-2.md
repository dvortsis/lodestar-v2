# X-ui-checks-2: overlay timings and radius, status pill states, the phone alert-and-Send flow (#PR)

Date: 2026-11-21Z. Decided by the build runner (split of `X-ui-checks`).

## Decisions

- **Overlay timings and radius.** A dialog and a bottom sheet are read on `/dev/ui/overlays`: computed animation name and duration while open and while leaving, and the four corner radii (16 px on a dialog, 16 px on a sheet's leading corners). The values come from `docs/design-tokens.md` §10.18 (`--duration-fast` 150 ms, `--duration-drawer-in` 300 ms, `--duration-drawer-out` 200 ms, `--radius-sheet` 16 px). The right-hand drawer and popovers are not measured.
- **Rebuild and paused states.** The rebuilding and idle pill states and the Search banner already have specs. This part adds the paused pill and the failed-rebuild pill, on a desktop and a phone width. The stale Insights banner already has `insights-stages.routes.test.ts`.
- **Phone alert-and-Send.** One touch spec at 390 px: Alerts list, one alert, Open in Search, tap a row, Send to the client, confirmation. A row on the alert page itself opens nothing; the torrent opens from Search.

## Not done

- **The layout-shift (CLS) check is dropped,** as the runner decided. Review 03 listed it; nothing here measures layout shift.
