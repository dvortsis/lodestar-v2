# X-ui-checks-2: overlay timings and radius, status pill states, the phone alert-and-Send flow (#1043)

Date: 2026-11-21. Decided by the build runner (split of `X-ui-checks`).

## Decisions

- **Overlay timings and radius.** Read live on `/dev/ui/overlays` (and `/dev/ui/feedback` for the toast): the computed animation name and duration, open and exit, and the four corner radii.
  - Dialog: 16 px all round; fades in over 150 ms and out over 200 ms.
  - Bottom sheet: 16 px on its top corners; slides in over 300 ms and out over 200 ms.
  - Drawer: 16 px on its start corners; slides in over 300 ms and out over 200 ms (a Svelte transition, so read from the page's `animate()` calls).
  - Popover and menu: 12 px; fade in over 150 ms and out over 100 ms.
  - Toast: 12 px; fades in over 200 ms (`--duration-banner`). It has no exit animation.
  - The sheet and drawer values come from `docs/design-tokens.md` (§6 radii, §9 durations). The dialog's fade is an implementation choice in `web/src/lib/ui/overlays/overlays.css` that the tokens don't spell out: it reuses `--duration-fast` in and `--duration-drawer-out` out, and the spec now holds it there. The exit is read from an `animationstart` listener (names ending `-out` only), because the panel is removed when the 200 ms exit ends.
- **Rebuild and paused states.** The rebuilding and idle pill states and the Search banner already have specs. This part adds the paused pill and the failed-rebuild pill, on a desktop and a phone width. On a phone the words are screen-reader text, so those cases check the pill's accessible name and that it fits the viewport. The stale Insights banner already has `insights-stages.routes.test.ts`.
- **Phone alert-and-Send.** One touch spec at 390 px: Alerts list, one alert, Open in Search, tap a row, Send to the client, confirmation. The spec goes by Open in Search. Tapping an alert row opens the torrent page directly (`/t/<hash>`); that path to Send is not walked here and is left out on purpose.

## Not done

- **The layout-shift (CLS) check is dropped,** as the runner decided. Review 03 listed it; nothing here measures layout shift.
