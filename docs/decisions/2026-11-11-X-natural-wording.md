# X-natural-wording decisions

- **The refusal counts tokens, so it says "words and symbols".** B7's cap (`MAX_NATURAL_WORDS`, 32) counts words, operators and brackets, so a phrase of 8 visible words in brackets can be refused. The API now builds its own `natural_too_long` text instead of interpolating `NaturalError`'s Display: "A natural search can hold at most 32 words and symbols; brackets and a lone minus sign count as one each. Shorten the search, or switch to the query language." The 512-byte case has its own sentence. A UX wording change under `docs/owner-decisions.md` §2.26 (runner's call); the cap itself and `crates/search/src/natural.rs` are unchanged.
- **The `/parse` doc comment and `openapi.json` say the same,** regenerated by the generator, not by hand.
- **Tests:** the golden `parse_natural_too_long.txt` is re-blessed; `natural_refusal_counts_symbols_not_just_words` refuses 8 visible words, one inside 13 nested pairs of brackets (34 tokens), and the 512-byte case is asserted. Both fail if the message goes back to interpolating `{e}`.
