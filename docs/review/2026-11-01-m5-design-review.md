# M5 design review (G-m5b), 2026-11-01

The `design-review` pass that gates M5 (architecture §10.17, §14 M5 row). The design decided with the owner comes first: CLAUDE.md "UI rules", §10, `docs/design-tokens.md`, `docs/ui-glossary.md`, and the decisions in `docs/decisions/`. `ui-design-skills` (applied by concept and spirit for Svelte) only decides what those leave open. How the review ran, and its limits, are in [How it ran](#how-it-ran).

**Result: 7 blocking, 10 important, 11 polish.** M5 can't exit until B1–B7 are fixed. B4 is already fixed in this PR; the rest go to FX-1 to FX-4. The fixes are grouped into six batches in [Fix batches](#fix-batches).

## Severity
- **Blocking:** breaks an owner decision or a §10 rule; fails contrast (§10.18) or axe; broken layout or overflow at a §10 breakpoint; unusable by keyboard; text that misleads.
- **Important:** a clear inconsistency or usability problem that doesn't break a rule.
- **Polish:** everything else.

## How it ran
- **Captures:** a local-only Playwright config re-ran the existing specs against the OpenAPI mock (`*.routes.test.ts`, `states.test.ts`, `smoke.test.ts`, and the `*.keyboard.test.ts` flows at 1024). A temporary auto fixture took a full-page screenshot at the end of each test and measured the page, so every state the suite already reaches was captured without writing new mocks. Neither the fixture nor the config is committed.
- **Projects:** 360, 1024 and 1920 (dark) on the full route set; 1920 light, 360 dark, 1024 dark, `rosewood-light`, `dracula-dark`, Compact and reduced motion on smoke + states + the table, bulk and Insights stage/print specs. That's 2,110 captures (some tests set their own width, for example "at 390 px"). The committed smoke and states baselines (every breakpoint × Riverstone light/dark) were reviewed too.
- **Measured on every capture:** horizontal page overflow (`scrollWidth − innerWidth`), visible elements past the viewport edge outside a scroll container, leaf text clipped by `overflow: hidden` without an ellipsis, and interactive elements under 24 px (and under 44 px in a `pointer: coarse` pass).
- **Static checks:** `scripts/check-bans.mjs` (hex, arbitrary values, off-ramp spacing) already gates the code. Greps for raw radii, weights, sizes and `rounded-full` found nothing outside `lib/theme`. A scan of `messages/en.json` against glossary §4 gave B7.
- **Touch pass:** an extra `coarse-390` project (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`) re-ran smoke, states, the Insights stage, search results/table and settings-form specs (80 tests) and flagged interactive elements under 44 × 44 px; that's B5. Labelled daisyUI checkboxes, radios and toggles (24 px) weren't counted against the rule where their `<label>` text makes the hit area larger.
- **Themes, density, motion:** the `rosewood-light`, `dracula-dark`, Compact and reduced-motion projects gave no findings beyond the ones above (same layouts, every state readable). Captured specs passed 2,064 of 2,110 (33 skipped). The 13 failures were desktop-only assertions run at 360 (for example clicking a nav link that is in the collapsed menu there), three layout-shift timing checks under load, and none was an axe failure.
- **What wasn't covered:** zh text width (not in the brief's matrix), 200 % zoom, and a real backend. Mock data drives every state, so counts and dates are fixtures. axe and the theme compiler's contrast gates are CI's: every captured spec that calls `expectNoA11yViolations` passed it in these projects. No contrast failure was found by eye in Rosewood or Dracula.
- **Screenshots:** `docs/review/m5-shots/`, cropped PNG8, 224 KB in total.
- **Self-review:** a fresh reviewer raised 2 blocking findings (the enum labels and the glossary copy, first filed as important and polish, are contract-doc breaches, now B6 and B7), 6 important (B2 missed the existing `EmptyState` primitive; B5's removals crossed batch files and its rule would have hit links in text; B1's cause is the shared Popover; I2 contradicted `2026-10-30-F-status.md` without saying so; the heading finding crossed into FX-3) and 6 polish. All are folded in; its suggestion to extend `check-bans.mjs` is P11.
