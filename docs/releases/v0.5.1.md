# Lodestar 0.5.1 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to install or update to Lodestar v0.5.1, the first public release. Two changes matter before you start: Lodestar now shows a Legal Notice at first-run setup, and its rule packs, the filters that hide malware, junk and copies made without permission, are on from the start. You review them during setup and can switch any off.

## What Changed

### New

- **Legal Notice:** setup shows a short notice about what Lodestar does on your network, what it finds and what is yours to decide. You tick a box to confirm you have read it. Settings → About keeps it, with the licences.
- **Unauthorised-copy rule pack:** hides torrents whose names follow the patterns of copies made without permission: camcorder recordings of films, cracked software, and scene-style film and TV rips. It can't know who holds the rights, so it also hides some lawful releases. An Allow rule brings one back.
- **Operator guide:** a page for anyone who lets other people reach their Lodestar, covering contact details, takedowns, reporting duties and personal data.

### Changed

- **Rule packs are on by default.** Setup shows every pack checked for review; you can uncheck any pack or rule there or in Settings → Filters & Safety. An install that already has Lodestar sees the review at the next sign-in.
- **The crawler waits on a fresh install** until setup is done, so your address doesn't join the network before you have read the notice.
- **Unraid listing:** the templates now sit under Network and Tools, since Lodestar isn't a download client.

### Fixed

- **Content filters fail closed:** when a filter can't load, Lodestar holds items back instead of letting them through.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone who installs or updates. Existing installs keep their settings; the only new step is the packs review at the next sign-in.

## Action Needed

From the release date (the release slice fills it in; this draft is dated 2026-11-04), before you open Lodestar for the first time after updating:

1. Read the Legal Notice and tick the box.
2. Review the rule packs. Leave them on unless you have a reason; use an Allow rule for a lawful release the unauthorised-copy pack hides.
3. If other people can reach your Lodestar, read the operator guide in `docs/legal/`.

## Known Issues

- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet. Add the template by hand, as Chapter 2 shows.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token into an issue. Report a security flaw through the repository's Security tab, privately.
