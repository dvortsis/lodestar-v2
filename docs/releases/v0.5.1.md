# Lodestar 0.5.1 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to install Lodestar v0.5.1, the first public release. One change matters before you start: Lodestar now shows a Legal Notice at first-run setup, and once after you update.

## What Changed

### New

- **Legal Notice:** setup shows a short notice about what Lodestar does on your network, what it finds and what is yours to decide. You tick a box to confirm you have read it. After an update, it shows once when you sign in. Settings → About shows it any time.
- **Operator guide:** for anyone who lets other people reach their Lodestar: contact details, takedowns, reporting duties, personal data.

### Changed

- **Unraid listing:** the templates now sit under Network and Tools, since Lodestar isn't a download client.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone who installs. If you ran a release candidate or a nightly build, back up before you switch. Your settings and filters stay as they are. Rule packs on by default and an unauthorised-copy pack come in v0.5.2.

## Action Needed

From the release date (this draft is dated 2026-11-05), before you open Lodestar for the first time:

1. Read the Legal Notice and tick the box.
2. If other people can reach your Lodestar, read the [operator guide](https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/blob/main/docs/legal/operator-guidance.md).

## Known Issues

- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet. Add the template by hand, as Chapter 2 shows.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.
- In-app updates aren't available yet. To update, back up, then pull the new image.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token into an issue. Report a security flaw through the repository's Security tab, privately.
