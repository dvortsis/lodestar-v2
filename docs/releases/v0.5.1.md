# Lodestar 0.5.1 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to install Lodestar v0.5.1, the first public release. Two changes matter before you start: Lodestar now shows a Legal Notice at first-run setup, and its rule packs are on from the start. You review them during setup.

## What Changed

### New

- **Legal Notice:** setup shows a short notice about what Lodestar does on your network, what it finds and what is yours to decide. You tick a box to confirm you have read it.
- **Unauthorised-copy rule pack:** hides torrents whose names follow the patterns of copies made without permission: camcorder recordings of films, cracked software, and scene-style film and TV rips. It can't know who holds the rights, so it also hides some lawful releases. An Allow rule brings one back. A malware-pack rule hides cracked software too, so turning this pack off doesn't restore it. On an updated install this pack starts off; the filters screen offers it. New rules inside packs you already use wait for a later version.
- **Operator guide:** for anyone who lets other people reach their Lodestar: contact details, takedowns, reporting duties, personal data.

### Changed

- **Rule packs are on by default.** Setup shows every pack checked for review; you can uncheck any pack there, and any rule in Settings → Filters & Safety. Updating from a nightly build? Your switches stay; the filters screen offers the rest, with a preview first.
- **Unraid listing:** the templates now sit under Network and Tools, since Lodestar isn't a download client.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone who installs. If you ran a nightly build, back up before you switch. Your filter settings stay as they are: packs you never turned on stay off, and the filters screen offers them with a preview first.

## Action Needed

From the release date (the release slice fills it in; this draft is dated 2026-11-04), before you open Lodestar for the first time after updating:

1. Read the Legal Notice and tick the box.
2. Review the rule packs. Leave them on unless you have a reason; use an Allow rule for a lawful release the unauthorised-copy pack hides.
3. If other people can reach your Lodestar, read the [operator guide](https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/blob/main/docs/legal/operator-guidance.md).

## Known Issues

- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet. Add the template by hand, as Chapter 2 shows.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.
- In-app updates aren't available yet. To update, back up, then pull the new image.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token into an issue. Report a security flaw through the repository's Security tab, privately.
