# Lodestar 0.5.2 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to update from v0.5.1. Rule packs are now on for new installs. The update upgrades your store, and v0.5.1 can't read the result, so back up first.

## What Changed

### New

- **Rule packs on by default (new installs):** setup shows every pack, and you can uncheck any of them. Unchecking asks for your password.
- **Unauthorised-copy pack:** hides camcorder rips, cracked software and scene-style releases. Internet Archive items and listed public-domain and Creative Commons films stay visible.
- **Spam clones:** a copy of a torrent the spam pack hid is hidden too.
- **Pack-rule overrides:** change one rule without turning off its pack, in Filters & Safety → Packs.
- **Sources screen:** Settings → Sources lists each source. Turning one off stops it at once.
- **Where a result came from:** the detail drawer shows its source.
- **In-app updates:** Settings → Updates, on Docker and Unraid.
- **Legal Notice v4:** shown once at your first sign-in after updating.

### Changed

- **Setup first:** a new install contacts no network until setup is done.
- **Faster searches:** file size and extension searches are quicker on large collections.
- **Store upgrade:** the update takes a snapshot, then moves your data to a newer format. v0.5.1 refuses the new format and changes nothing.
- **Search rebuild:** the index is rebuilt once, in the background. Search keeps working, but the rebuild needs about twice the index's disk space.
- **Re-classification:** in the background, about a minute per million torrents. Nothing is filtered differently meanwhile.
- **Safer logs:** passwords and tokens in web addresses reach logs in fewer cases.
- **Private finds stay private:** an imported torrent marked private is never looked up on the public DHT.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone updating from v0.5.1. Your packs stay as you set them; new ones are offered, never switched on.

## Action Needed

From Nov. 7, 2026 (rc.1):

1. Back up your data folder, then pull the new image.
2. Sign in and read the Legal Notice.
3. Open Filters & Safety and decide which packs to turn on.

## Known Issues

- Tracker scraping is on by default, as in v0.5.1. It asks public trackers for peer counts.
- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- Sonarr and Radarr webhooks are advanced: off by default, and their token is made through the API only.
- The torrent cache, Internet Archive and Sukebei are built but can't be turned on yet.
- In-app updates aren't available on Windows.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet. Add the template by hand.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token. Report a security flaw privately through the repository's Security tab.
