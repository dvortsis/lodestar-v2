# Lodestar 0.5.2 Release Notes

## Summary

Rule packs are now on for new installs. The update upgrades your store, and v0.5.1 can't read the result, so back up first.

## What Changed

### New

- **Rule packs on by default (new installs):** setup lets you uncheck any; unchecking asks for your password.
- **Unauthorised-copy pack:** hides camcorder rips, cracked software and scene-style releases. Internet Archive items and listed public-domain and Creative Commons films stay visible.
- **Spam clones:** a copy of a torrent the spam pack hid is hidden too. Updated installs get it after accepting it.
- **Pack-rule overrides:** change one rule without turning off its pack, in Filters & Safety → Packs.
- **Sources screen:** Settings → Sources lists each source; turning one off stops it at once.
- **Where a result came from:** the detail drawer shows its source.
- **In-app updates:** Settings → Updates (Docker and Unraid).
- **Legal Notice v4:** shown once at your first sign-in after updating.

### Changed

- **Setup first:** a new install contacts no network until setup is done.
- **Faster searches:** by file size and extension, on large collections.
- **Store upgrade:** after a snapshot, your data moves to a newer format that v0.5.1 can't open.
- **Search rebuild:** once, in the background. Search keeps working, but it needs about twice the index's disk space.
- **Re-classification:** in the background, about a minute per million torrents; filtering is unchanged meanwhile.
- **Safer logs:** passwords and tokens in web addresses reach logs in fewer cases.
- **Privacy:** imported private torrents are never looked up on the public DHT.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone updating from v0.5.1. Your packs stay as you set them; new packs and rules wait for you to accept them.

## Action Needed

From the v0.5.2-rc.2 tag (Nov. 7, 2026 at the earliest):

1. Back up your data folder ([section 3.7](https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/blob/main/docs/user/03-keep-lodestar-safe.md#37-back-up-and-restore)), then pull the new image. On Windows, run the new installer.
2. Sign in and read the Legal Notice.
3. In Filters & Safety, choose your packs and review new rules waiting in packs you use.

## Known Issues

- Tracker scraping is on by default, as in v0.5.1: it asks public trackers for peer counts.
- Seeder and tracker filters aren't available yet.
- Sonarr and Radarr webhooks are advanced: off by default, and their token is made through the API only.
- The torrent cache, Internet Archive and Sukebei are built but can't be turned on yet.
- In-app updates aren't available on Windows.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet; add the template by hand.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token. Report a security flaw privately through the repository's Security tab.
