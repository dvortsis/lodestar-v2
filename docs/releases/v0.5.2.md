# Lodestar 0.5.2 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to update from v0.5.1. Three things matter before you start: rule packs are now on for new installs, a store upgrade means you can't go back without a backup, and some features listed here are still pending.

## What Changed

### New

- **Rule packs on by default (new installs):** a new data folder starts with every pack on, and setup lets you review and uncheck any of them. Unchecking asks for your password.
- **Unauthorised-copy pack:** hides camcorder rips, cracked software and scene-style releases, except from official sources. <!-- pending: RP-UC -->
- **Pack-rule overrides:** change how a single rule in a pack behaves, without turning off the pack.
- **Sources screen:** Settings → Sources lists each source and lets you switch it on or off.
- **Sonarr and Radarr webhooks:** send finds to Lodestar from your own apps.
- **Where a result came from:** the detail drawer shows its source. <!-- pending: SRC-17 -->
- **Legal Notice v3:** you see it once when you sign in after updating. <!-- pending: LEGAL-1 -->

### Changed

- **Faster file searches:** searching by file extension and size is quicker on large collections. <!-- pending: X-search-files-4 -->
- **Store upgrade:** the store moves from V12 to V14. Lodestar takes a snapshot first. v0.5.1 refuses a newer store and changes nothing, so to go back, restore your backup.
- **Re-classification:** after the update, Lodestar re-classifies stored torrents in the background, about a minute per million. Search results aren't filtered differently meanwhile. <!-- pending: CLASSIFY_VERSION 3 to 4 -->
- **Safer logs:** web addresses with passwords or tokens no longer leak into logs.

## Who Is Affected

Everyone who updates from v0.5.1. If you already use Lodestar, your packs stay as you set them. You are offered the packs, never switched.

## Action Needed

From the release date (not yet set; this draft is dated 2026-11-05):

1. Back up your data folder, then pull the new image.
2. Sign in and read the Legal Notice.
3. Open Filters & Safety and decide which packs to turn on.

## Known Issues

- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- In-app updates aren't available yet.
- Internet Archive, Sukebei and torrent-cache sources are built but stay off until their checks pass. They come later.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token. Report a security flaw through the repository's Security tab, privately.
