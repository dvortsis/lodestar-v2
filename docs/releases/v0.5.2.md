# Lodestar 0.5.2 Release Notes

## Summary

Use this to decide whether to update from v0.5.1. Three things matter before you start: rule packs are now on for new installs, a store upgrade means you can't go back without a backup, and some features listed here are still pending.

## What Changed

### New

- **Rule packs on by default (new installs):** a new data folder starts with every pack on, and setup lets you review and uncheck any of them. Unchecking asks for your password.
- **Unauthorised-copy pack:** hides camcorder rips, cracked software and scene-style releases. Internet Archive items and listed public-domain and Creative Commons films stay visible. <!-- pending: RP-UC -->
- **Pack-rule overrides:** change one rule in a pack without turning off the pack. API only; the screen comes later.
- **Sources screen:** Settings → Sources lists each source and switches it on or off.
- **Where a result came from:** the detail drawer shows its source. <!-- pending: SRC-17 -->
- **In-app updates:** Settings → Updates on Docker and Unraid. <!-- pending: P-v052-cut checks the release image runs under its supervisor -->
- **Legal Notice v3:** you see it once when you sign in after updating. <!-- pending: LEGAL-1 -->

### Changed

- **Faster size searches:** searching files by size is quicker on large collections.
- **Faster extension searches:** the same for file extensions. <!-- pending: #468 -->
- **Store upgrade:** the update moves your data to a newer store format, after a snapshot. v0.5.1 refuses it and changes nothing, so to go back, restore your backup.
- **Re-classification:** in the background after the update, about a minute per million torrents. Nothing is filtered differently meanwhile. <!-- pending: CLASSIFY_VERSION 3 to 4 -->
- **Safer logs:** web addresses with passwords or tokens leak into logs in fewer cases. <!-- pending: X-log-url-redact-2 -->

## Who Is Affected

Everyone who updates from v0.5.1. If you already use Lodestar, your packs stay as you set them. You are offered the packs, never switched.

## Action Needed

From the release date (not yet set; this draft is dated 2026-11-05):

1. Back up your data folder, then pull the new image.
2. Sign in and read the Legal Notice. <!-- pending: LEGAL-1 -->
3. Open Filters & Safety and decide which packs to turn on.

## Known Issues

- Seeder and tracker filters still say **Not available yet**.
- Sonarr and Radarr webhooks are built but not connected yet. They come later.
- Unraid's Community Applications doesn't list Lodestar yet. Add the template by hand.
- The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen warns. Check the file's checksum first.
- In-app updates aren't available on Windows.
- Internet Archive, Sukebei and torrent-cache sources are built but stay off until their checks pass. They come later.

## Get Help

Report a problem at <https://github.com/dvortsis/lodestar-v2/issues>. Don't paste logs with torrent details or your setup token. Report a security flaw through the repository's Security tab, privately.
