## Confirmed OK
- **Item 1** (`siblings.ts`):
  - Parsed and Related join one flight per hash, and a later mount reuses the kept answer.
  - The kept answer is keyed by hash and checked against the content generation (`detailGeneration`, bumped by `clearDetailCaches` on content settings, resync and sign-in/out) and `FRESH_MS`. That is the same rule as the existing detail cache, so nothing leaks across torrents or settings changes.
  - Only ready answers are kept.
  - Each of these is unit-tested in `siblings.test.ts`: a wrong-hash answer is `failed` and never kept; a 404 is `withheld` (Related removes the section, Parsed shows nothing of it); a stale-generation answer is dropped; the last unsubscribe aborts and removes the flight.
