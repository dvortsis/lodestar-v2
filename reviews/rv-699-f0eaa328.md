## b3eb64d4: the send loop reads the session before the generation
- **The order is correct.** `connected` does `fetch_add(AcqRel)` before `send_replace`, and the watch's lock makes the bump visible to a reader that sees the new session. So a new session always comes with its new generation, and it's reset exactly once. That removes the old second reset, which re-sent replayed batches under new ids.
- **The reverse skew is safe.** The loop can see a stale session with the new generation, but only if `disconnected` and `connected` both land in the few instructions between the two reads. The old session has ended by then. With a spool, `spool_live` writes the live batches to disk and they replay later; without one, `send_due` returns early and the batches stay unsent. Either way they go out on the new session, so nothing is lost or duplicated.
- **No lock is held across an await.** The `borrow_and_update()` guard is dropped at the end of its statement.
