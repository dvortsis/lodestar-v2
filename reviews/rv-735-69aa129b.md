## Blocking

At 1 KB, about 320 B of growth would pass without anyone noticing. Companions' 1 KB is not a reason to repeat it.
   - **Fix:** `'0.75 KB'`. While editing, update the comment above it (`:1380`): parts measure 26.32 kB at this head, not 25.71 KB, so the 27 KB limit is about +2.6%, not about +5%. That's fine, but the comment should say what's true.

## Important
1. **`logic.ts:56-66` (`ourExport`): the export job is matched by the browser's clock against the server's `started_at`, with only 5 s of slack.** The two clocks are often skewed: a NAS or a container without NTP, or a phone.
   - **Browser ahead by more than 5 s:** the new job never matches, and after 30 s the screen says "couldn't find this export's progress" even though the export is working.
   - **Browser behind:** a bitmagnet export that finished shortly before can match on the first poll. `keepPolling` then stops at once because that job's `finished_at` is set, so the screen shows the old job's "Done: N torrents" for the new download.
   - **Fix:** match by job id, not time. Read `/jobs` when the screen mounts (or on hover or focus of the download link), remember the highest export job id, and take the newest bitmagnet export with a higher id. If the remembered id is missing, fall back to the time check.
   - It's a cheap fix, so do it in this PR if possible. Otherwise put it in the next brief that touches Outputs.
2. **`Bitmagnet.svelte:107-111`: if `/jobs` fails three times after the job was already found, `lost` turns on and the screen says it "couldn't find this export's progress".** That wording is wrong once a job is known. Keep showing the last known state and add "progress stopped updating; see Jobs", or say "lost" only when `job === null`. This is small and cosmetic, but it's a wrong message.
