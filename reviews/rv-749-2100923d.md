## Should fix
1. **`break-all` splits the very placeholder the user has to replace.** With `word-break: break-all` the browser breaks at the line's last character even when a space is available. At 1366 px the command renders as `…ndjson http://<bitmagn` / `et-host>:3333/import`, and at 360 px as `…ndjson htt` / `p://<bitmagnet-host>:3333/import`. The new sentence tells a novice to "Replace <bitmagnet-host>", and on a desktop that token is cut in half. That works against "reads whole at every width".
   I swapped in Tailwind 4's `wrap-anywhere` (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`, a standard utility that check-bans accepts) and ran the same probe. It still never overflows at 320/360/1366, and it also counts toward min-content, so it is as safe as `break-all` inside flex/grid. It breaks at spaces and hyphens first:
   - 1366: `curl --data-binary @lodestar-export-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.bitmagnet.ndjson` / `http://<bitmagnet-host>:3333/import`
   - 360: `curl --data-binary @lodestar-export-` / `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.bitmagnet.ndjson` / `http://<bitmagnet-host>:3333/import`

   It's a one-word change (`break-all` → `wrap-anywhere`). The new e2e passes with it unchanged. Also update the commit/PR wording and the inline comment if you change it. `break-all` stays right for single tokens such as hashes (`Mono.svelte`, `InstallDialog`). A command line has natural break points, so `break-all` is the weaker choice here.
