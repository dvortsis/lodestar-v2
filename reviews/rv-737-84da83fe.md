# rv-737 at 84da83fe: X-wk-out-set-polish

**Verdict: MERGE.** Nothing blocks. One Important item, which is a gap in the reasoning plus a cheap fix, and four Nits.

## Important
1. **The 60 s grace rests on a wrong premise.** See `crates/pipeline/src/worker.rs:51-53,78` and `docs/decisions/2026-11-12-X-wk-out-set-polish.md:9`.
   - **The claim:** "milliseconds apart in the runtime".
   - **What the code does:**
     - The worker's `committed` fires on `ToWorker::Accepted`.
     - `Accepted` only means the batch went into the coordinator's 16-slot intake queue (`remote.rs:1099-1108`).
     - The coordinator's lease ends only when its writer commits (`remote.rs:1120-1121`, "which the commit ends"; `feed` at `:1033-1060` waits on the 64-slot writer queue).
   - **Failure scenario:** the coordinator's writer stalls for more than 60 s (busy SQLite, slow disk).
     - The holder's claim is answered `Held` with the still-live lease.
     - The worker's entry is gone, so the answer reads as `New`. The pool's `recent` check then calls `Funnel::duplicate`, and the lease ends as lost.
     - That is #727's bug, back in that window. #727 itself covered 1,200 s.
   - **Fix:**
     - State the real window: from `Accepted` to the coordinator's writer commit.
     - Then either size `FINISHED_GRACE` against a bound on the writer's lag, or raise it to something like 180 s. At 250/s that's about 45k entries, near the cap, so restate the memory figures.
     - Add the residual window to the WK-G note.

## Nits
- **Cap eviction order** (`worker.rs:91,133-136`):
  - `finished` is keyed by seq alone. At the cap, a lost entry that needs the horizon goes before a committed one that needs only the grace.
  - Fix: key `finished` by `(expires, seq)`.
  - This only bites above about 830 commits/s.
- **`later`'s fallback** (`worker.rs:319-322`): `now + 100 y` can itself panic when `checked_add` failed for a shorter `by`.
  - Fix: `now.checked_add(by).or_else(|| now.checked_add(100y)).unwrap_or(now)`.
  - It can't happen in practice.
- **The decisions heading** (`docs/decisions/2026-11-12-X-wk-out-set-polish.md:1`) lacks "(#737)". This is the known nit.
- **The commit message of 1e41e38dc** still says "1-2 MB … 7.5 MB at most". The later commit and the PR body have the right figures.
