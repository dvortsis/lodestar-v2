## Checked and correct

### 1. Consent and switches unchanged
- `git diff origin/main...pr/816` shows no change to `ConsentDialog.svelte`, `consent.ts`, `consent-approved.fixture.ts`, `PurgeDialog.svelte`, `ConfigDialog.svelte` or `dialogs.test.ts`. The §2.49 strings live there and in the approved fixture.

- In the message files, the only `-` lines are the old last entry being re-added with a trailing comma. No existing string changed.

### 2. No back door through collapsed or filtered rows
- **A switch always goes through its dialog.** Every enable still goes through `toggled()`. Account opens its dialog on `s.consent === 'account'` whatever `need` is, so the `setupNeed` change can't let an Account enable skip it.
- **Closed rows and groups.** A closed group or row uses `hidden`, so its controls can't be reached. Tailwind v4's preflight sets `[hidden]` to `display: none !important`, which beats `flex`, and the e2e asserts `toBeHidden()` in a real browser.
- **Keyboard.** The row's expand button sits inside the `h3`, next to the `Toggle` rather than around it. Its `onclick` only flips `open`, so Enter or Space can't reach the switch.
- **No orphaned dialog.** The dialog's `target` comes from `sources`, not from the filtered `shown` list. The bits-ui modal traps focus, and a click outside closes it first, so the filter can't change while a dialog is open. After close, focus goes back to the switch that opened it.
- **Setup note.** The note the switch points to through `aria-describedby` stays on the line, outside the hidden detail, so it's always in the page.
- **Endpoints.** The addresses moved into the detail, but `ConsentDialog.svelte:198-201` still lists them in the dialog itself, so an acknowledgement still shows what it covers.
- **Tested.** The layout e2e toggles Nyaa after filtering, sees the unchanged External dialog, presses Escape, and asserts the switch is unchecked and `sent` is empty.

### 3. Untrusted text renders safely
- No `{@html}`, `innerHTML` or `insertAdjacentHTML` anywhere in `features/sources`, `Input`, `EmptyState` or `Badge`.
- Names, addresses and `last_error` are text interpolation or paraglide parameters.
- `matches()` uses `String.includes` on lower-cased text: no regex, so no ReDoS or injection. The query never leaves the page.
- Element ids come from kind enums and numeric source ids, never from user text.

### 4. Accessibility
