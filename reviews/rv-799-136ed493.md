- **O2.** `apply_inner`'s `check_plan` and `read_have` failures (flaresolverr.rs:744-745) skip `failed()`, so they emit no `config_failed` event. `plan` and each step do emit one. Wrap them for parity.

## Security (all verified, nothing blocking)
- **Opt-in holds.**
  - The entry has `opt_in: true` and is `challenge_solver` only, and the loader refuses it otherwise (tested).
  - It's not in `ADAPTERS`, `auto_probes` or `Guided::detect`. `set_target` is fixed to qBittorrent (`HANDED_OVER`).
  - `Switch::Off` is the default, and nothing outside the crate calls `pairing` or `find`.
  - Pins and digest are null, so manager and templates refuse it (`awaiting_pin`). No native profile exists (`NoProfile`).
- **Probes are bounded.**
  - Detection and health go through `CompanionHttp` within `Limits::PROBE` (5 s, 64 KiB). Prowlarr calls use 10 s and 256 KiB.
  - `find` probes only the catalogue's fixed loopback and container-name targets.
- **No entered URL becomes the address.** `SolverAddress::for_prowlarr` refuses an entered target and a loopback/container mix. The URL is built from a typed `PortNumber` and `ContainerName`.
- **Writes are guarded and the key stays out of logs.**
  - Consent is checked before plan and apply, and nothing is sent without it (tested).
  - The key goes only in `X-Api-Key` via `HeaderValue::secret`. Log events carry step names only, and the tests assert the key never appears in log lines or paths.
- **Untrusted JSON is parsed with bounds.**
  - The bounded reader has explicit limits. Escaped and duplicate keys are refused, and ids are strict `u32`.
  - Proptests over random bytes and JSON-shaped noise pass. Oversized and malformed answers are tested.
- **The port stays off the LAN.** 8191 is internal, nothing is published by default, and a LAN publish is refused in compose and Unraid (tested). There's no host network. `NATIVE_BIND` is `HOST=127.0.0.1`.
- **A proxy you made is never touched.** Lodestar's proxy must have the name, the FlareSolverr implementation and the tag. `Off` deletes only Lodestar's (tested).
