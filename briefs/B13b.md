# B13b: siblings for short no-year titles via the title-identity key
**Model:** Opus (search core). Fresh Opus reviewer follows. Runner brief, 2026-11-05T18:55Z. Same session as X-search-files-5.

## Why
B13 (`docs/decisions/2026-10-22-B13.md`, Known limit) finds siblings from a 1,000-candidate window. Short titles with no year ("Up", "Heat") have so many candidates that their real siblings can fall outside it. X-search-files-5 (#505, merged a1e0d849) adds the `title_identity` field (an exact keyword of the normalised title plus spans) in search SCHEMA_VERSION 7. B13b uses that field.

## Do (branch `slice/B13b` from `origin/main`)
1. **Sibling lookup by identity.** In `crates/api/src/compare.rs` (and `discover.rs` if siblings are also built there), look up siblings with an exact `title_identity` term query first, then fall back to today's window. The ranking and output shape stay as they are today. Sibling results must keep every filter, hide, adult and visibility rule the current path applies. Reuse the same search path; don't add a bypass.
2. **One rule, not two.** `api::compare::identity` and `search::doc::title_identity` must not drift. Have compare derive the identity from the search crate's function, or expose one shared function. If that's impossible, add a cross-crate test over a corpus of names that asserts they agree.
3. **Population contract.** On an index where `title_identity` is Missing or Backfilling (an older index, or mid-rebuild), use today's window. Never treat an unpopulated field as "no siblings". Decide per snapshot, as #505's I-1 fix does. Test the Backfilling fallback; it isn't reachable at generation 1, so use `Generations::with("title_identity", 2)` or the equivalent, as #505's tests do.
4. **Tests:**
   - "Up" and "Heat" with more than 1,000 decoy candidates still find their siblings;
   - a hidden or adult sibling is still withheld exactly as today;
   - the identity agreement test from step 2;
   - the fallback on an older or backfilling index.

   No fixed sleeps.
5. **Decisions file:** `docs/decisions/2026-11-05-B13b.md`. Add the `runner/slices.yaml` entry (`B13b`, deps `[B13, X-search-files-5]`) and its placement.
