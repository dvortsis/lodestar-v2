## Do
1. **The segment format** (`crates/pipeline/src/spool.rs`): append-only segments with a versioned header (magic, version, worker id, epoch or lease-epoch marker as you need), length-prefixed, checksummed records, each record one encoded `Results` or `Counters` payload with its batch id and fencing tokens. Bounded decoder: every length checked against the remaining bytes and a per-record cap before allocating. A torn or corrupt final record is dropped, never half-used; corruption in the middle stops that segment (counted, logged by name only).
2. **Sealing at rest (owner wkq1):** segments are sealed with the worker's own `SecretStore` (`<data>/secrets.key`), and `--rotate-key` reseals them before the retire. Removal once acknowledged uses secure deletion as §2.17 asks for the store (overwrite then unlink; say in the decisions file what that does and doesn't give on SSDs and on Windows).
3. **No peer addresses** in any spool record (§18.7, §19.6). A test proves it.
4. **Caps:** at most 1 GB or 24 h (defaults; configurable only within those bounds unless the plan says otherwise). Full means fetching pauses and the DHT keeps only routing. Records older than 24 h are dropped and counted, not replayed.
5. **Writes are atomic and durable:** a segment is created under a temp name, fsynced, renamed; appends fsync at a bounded cadence; this works on Windows (rename over an open file, sharing modes) as well as Linux.
6. **Replay on reconnect** (`crates/lodestar/src/worker/spool.rs`): in order, idempotent by batch id and token; a segment is deleted once every batch in it is acknowledged (`accepted: true`, whatever the commit decided, per WK-9). Backpressure (`accepted: false`) retries later; replay never blocks live heartbeats.
7. **Coordinator refusal (Q7):** a replay from a worker that is quarantined, revoked or purged, or any record older than its last purge, is refused whatever the epoch. Check what WK-9 already does; if the coordinator needs a change in `crates/pipeline/src/remote.rs` or `crates/lodestar/src/cluster/intake.rs`, ask the runner first (likely approved as a shared edit).
8. **Lease-count reconciliation** on reconnect (V6-cluster2 "lease-count drift"): the worker and coordinator agree on outstanding leases after a replay; drift is counted.
9. **Metrics:** `spool_bytes` in the heartbeat stats, plus the spool's own metric rows in `docs/metrics.md` (bytes, records, dropped-old, dropped-torn, paused).
10. **Fuzz target** `fuzz/fuzz_targets/worker_spool.rs` on the segment decoder, plus its `[[bin]]` in `fuzz/Cargo.toml`. Property tests for encode/decode round trip.
11. **Decisions file** `docs/decisions/2026-11-11-WK-11.md`: the format, the caps, secure deletion's limits, the wire-key-day question below, and any refinement flagged (§2.19).

**Decide and record:** a sealed record spooled under day N's wire key and replayed after the day boundary. WK-9c makes an unopenable record `Invalid`. Either the coordinator keeps enough old wire keys to open a 24 h spool, or such records are requeued/re-fetched; pick the one that never loses a key silently and never keeps a key longer than the spool cap needs, and say why.
